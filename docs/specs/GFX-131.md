# GFX-131: Qingshi's heart

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's village squares and Stardew Valley's town centre (composition and mechanics only, never
their art or layouts).

## Why (planner shots 2026-11-13 06:10, hud_arrival.png / hud_after_sect.png at 1280x720)
- Qingshi's north-south road (`map.paths` `[770, 0, 60, 1000]`) reads as **railway tracks**: two dark edge strips and two ruts run
  unbroken from top to bottom of the screen. The east-west road has the same pieces but reads better only because it is short on
  screen. The Winter market street has the same problem with its two slab columns (GFX-128 Follow-up).
- The crossroads, where the bounty board, the herb seller, the wandering merchant and the temple gather, is plain lawn. A village
  has a heart: a packed-earth square with a big old tree people meet under.
- Trees are sprinkled evenly as round blobs (qingshi `decor` has no `tree_kinds` or `clusters`).

## What to build
1. **Roads that wander** (`Scenery.path_tiles`, the grass/litter branch, `src/core/systems/scenery.gd` ~line 600):
   - Edge strips: instead of 40 px segments of a fixed 3 px strip, lay segments 10-28 px long with 0-8 px gaps, thickness 2-4 px,
     and an inward/outward offset of 0-2 px per segment (seeded rng already in the function). Some gaps get a `tuft` creeping in.
   - Ruts: segments 14-36 px with 4-12 px gaps; each segment shifts sideways by -2..+2 px from the last, so the line wobbles.
     Within ~40 px of a crossing with another `map.paths` rect, drop ruts entirely (the square in step 2 covers it).
   - Paved `main_street` (market): stagger the two slab rows by half a slab, and every 4th slab (seeded) spans the full width,
     so a vertical street never shows two unbroken columns.
   - Keep all GFX-124 rules (winter covers ruts except the trodden centre, puddles on wet days, nothing on bridges or place keepouts).
2. **The village square**: new optional `map.square: [x, y, w, h]` (document in data/regions.json `_doc`, validate it is four
   numbers with w,h > 0 in `Scenery.validate`). Set it for qingshi_village around the crossroads (start near `[620, 400, 360, 200]`
   and adjust after looking at the shot; it must not cover the pond or the fields).
   New pure `Scenery.village_square(region: Dictionary) -> Dictionary` (own rng seeded `hash("%s|square" % id)`, no GameState):
   - `outline: PackedVector2Array`: `jagged_outline(rect, seed, 18, 10.0)`, a soft-edged patch of packed earth.
   - `patches: Array[Dictionary]` `{rect, shade}`: 10-16 worn darker/lighter earth patches inside it.
   - `tree: Dictionary` `{pos: Vector2, r: float}`: one big old tree (canopy radius ~44) with a stone ring, placed in the square
     where it overlaps no place keepout (`Npcs.place_keepout(pl).grow(12)`, plus a 26 px label band above), no path rect, no
     arrival ring; `{}` if no spot fits.
   - `props: Array[Dictionary]` `{kind, rect}`: 1-2 `bench` and 1 `lantern_post` pair, same keep-clear rules (use `is_clear`).
   Draw it in `src/world/world.gd` in the ground pass **before** the roads (roads stay on top), with the same walked-earth palette
   as GFX-124/GFX-127 yards; the tree after landmarks with its shadow (`tree_shadow`), seasonal canopy via `tree_look` (round kind;
   blossoms in spring, gold in autumn, bare with snow in winter). Under snow the square takes the snowy road tint.
3. **Groves**: give qingshi `decor` `"tree_kinds": {"round": 3, "willow": 1, "bamboo": 1}` and `"clusters": 3` (both already
   supported, see Misty Forest/market), keeping the tree count. Check that no tree lands on the square.

## Tests (tests/unit/test_village_square.gd, plus additions to the existing path_tiles tests)
- `path_tiles` on a 60x1000 vertical grass path and a 1600x60 horizontal one: no `edge` or `rut` rect is longer than 40 px along
  the road; each edge side has at least one gap in every 120 px; vertical and horizontal give piece counts within 25% of each other.
- Market main street: no two consecutive slabs in the same row share the same start offset as the other row (staggered).
- `village_square`: deterministic; the tree and props overlap no place keepout, path rect or each other; `{}`/empty for a
  region without `map.square`; `Scenery.validate` rejects `"square": [1, 2]`.
