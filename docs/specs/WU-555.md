# WU-555: Names that step aside

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's town labels (look only: a person's name never sits on a building's name).

## Problem
Planner shots 2026-11-16 18:10, Misty Forest at 1280x800: "Herbalist Lan" touches "Herb Patch" in summer and lies on top of
it in winter; at Azure Peak "Yun Feng" crowds "Cloud-Stream Ford". `Interactable.clear_person_labels` and `spread_labels`
(src/world/interactables/interactable.gd ~244-268, called once from world.gd ~834) lay labels out when the region is
built, but NPCs then wander (`npc.gd` `_physics_process` ~166: `position = home + _wander + bob`, up to WANDER_RADIUS) and
roamers walk, and their labels move with them onto place labels.

## What to build
1. A person's label is laid out against **place** labels only (places never move): keep a cached list of place label rects
   in the world (build it where `clear_person_labels` runs; refresh on rebuild).
2. When a person (NPC or roamer) has moved more than 6 px since its label was last placed, re-run
   `LabelLayout.person_offset` for that one label against the cached place rects (with WU-549's `LABEL_MIN_GAP` on the
   row), at most every 0.2 s per person (a timer/accumulator, not every frame). Ease the label to its new offset over
   ~0.15 s (instant with `MotionFx.reduced()`) so it doesn't jump.
3. Ignore the vertical bob when deciding whether the person moved (use `home + _wander`, not `position`).
4. Person-vs-person overlap stays as today (spread at build); don't add per-frame person-person solving.
5. Keep WU-522's rule: a person's label may rise but never drops onto their own head; WU-511's "drops below their feet"
   option still applies.

## Tests (tests/unit, extend test_label_layout.gd or the closest existing label test)
- A static helper (e.g. `Interactable.relayout_person(node, place_rects)` or a pure `LabelLayout` function) is unit-tested:
  a person rect overlapping a place rect gets an offset that clears it by >= LABEL_MIN_GAP on the row.
- An NPC built next to a place, moved by 40 px toward the place label: after one relayout its drawn label rect does not
  intersect the place's label rect.
- Movement under 6 px (or bob only) does not trigger a relayout.

## Acceptance
- Render `--region=misty_forest` (Summer and Winter) and `--region=azure_peak --season=Winter` at 1280x800 and open them:
  no NPC or roamer name touches a place name. Also run the default first-hour shots: Qingshi labels as good as before.
- `tools/test.sh` passes. Draw-only, no balance check.
