# WU-567: Find the one you're looking for (goal marker)

Role: world-ui. Reference: Tale of Immortal's quest tracker and Wandering Sword's town markers (mechanics only: a small
marker over the person the current goal is about; no names, layouts or art copied).

## Why
WU-566's arrival shot (1280x800): the Goal line says "Talk to Elder Mo", but nothing on screen marks him. Worse, the
"Meditation Rock" label (regions.json qingshi_village place at [555, 355]) sits over Elder Mo's sprite, and his own
"Elder Mo" label floats ~60 px above the figure. A new player cannot see who to walk to.

## What to build
1. **Goal targets in core.** `Guidance.first_goals()` rows gain an optional `target` dictionary:
   - "Talk to Elder Mo ..." -> `{"npc": "elder_mo"}`
   - "Learn a fighting style" -> `{"npc": <the nearest teacher Guidance already names in its "No fighting style:" hint>}`
     (reuse that hint's lookup; empty when none).
   - fish/field row -> `{"place": <display name of the fishing spot>}`; cook row -> `{"place": <workshop display name>}`.
   - others: no target.
   Add `static func goal_target(c, data, flags) -> Dictionary` returning the first unfinished goal's target (or `{}`; also
   `{}` once `first_goals_done`). Pure, unit-tested in `tests/unit/test_guidance*.gd` (Mo before the talk flag, `{}` after
   all goals, the fighting-style teacher id).
2. **The marker (src/world).** A small `GoalMarker` node (new script `src/world/goal_marker.gd`, `##` doc comment, typed)
   drawn in `_draw()`: a gold downward chevron (~9x7 px at world scale, 1 px dark outline) bobbing 2 px over the target's
   head (NPC) or the place's top edge. It follows a walking NPC every frame. Only in the target's current region; refresh on
   the same signals the HUD Next-steps card refreshes on (goal changes, travel). `reduce_motion`: no bob.
   When the target is in the region but off screen, reuse `BeastArrow.edge_arrow()` for a gold edge arrow with the short name
   (a second instance of BeastArrow tinted gold is fine if it supports a colour + name; otherwise a tiny subclass).
3. **Mo's labels.** Fix the label overlap in the arrival view: the NPC name label must sit just above the sprite (it
   currently floats ~60 px high: check `LabelLayout.person_offset` / the nudge in world.gd and why it pushes this one up),
   and a place label (Meditation Rock) must never cover a person sprite: add person sprite rects as `covers`/blockers for
   place labels in the existing `LabelLayout.resolve` call. If moving Elder Mo's haunt a few px is the clean fix, do it in data.

## Tests
- Guidance unit tests above.
- A world test (like the existing label tests, e.g. grep `LabelLayout` in tests/unit) that builds Qingshi on day 0 and asserts
  the goal marker exists over elder_mo and that no place label rect intersects Elder Mo's sprite rect.

## Acceptance
Render `screenshot_first_hour.gd` (default, Qingshi day 0) and `--arrival-card` at 1280x800 and open them: the gold chevron is
over Elder Mo, his name sits right above him, "Meditation Rock" doesn't cover him. After talking to him (any shot that sets the
flag, or `--flag=talked_elder_mo`) the marker moves to the next goal's target or disappears. tools/test.sh green. < ~300 lines.
