# QA-145: Does following the far-qi clause change the first years?

## Why
Every recent trace (seeds 43, 47, 53, 59, 61) stays in Qingshi for 12 months. From about QR 7 every fight near home is
100% (seed 61: 45 won / 3 lost), and at Foundation the Gather line says Azure Peak's Cloud-Sea Cliff would take 1 month,
24 days instead of 4 months, 13 days. Nothing in the sim ever takes that trip. Before GUIDE-128 makes the trip the
headline, measure what it does (pacing is the owner's open question F-005d).

## Changes (sim only)
1. `tests/sim/first_hour.gd`: `static var far_qi := false`. When true, once a month before meditating: call
   `Guidance.far_qi_offer(c, data, gs.current_region, gs.world_flags)` if GUIDE-128 is on main; otherwise parse today's
   Gather line for " in <Region> (N days away)" and resolve it with `Guidance.hinted_region`. If an offer exists and
   saves >= 30 days net of the round trip, travel there (`gs.travel` along `Guidance.first_hop` until arrived, as
   `_buy_breakthrough_pill` does), meditate at the best spot there for the rest of the month (`GameState.cultivate` with
   that density), and stay: the new region becomes home for later months (sect missions need no travel). Count
   trips, days travelled, and the month the player moved.
2. `tests/sim/simulate_first_hour.gd`: `--far-qi` (add to the argument filter). Print
   `Far qi (QA-145): moved in N/10 seeds, median month M, days on the road D`.
3. Also run `tests/sim/simulate_curious_years.gd` with the same flag if it shares FirstHour's policy (it does if it calls
   FirstHour.play; check), 10 seeds x 36 months.

## Report (commit message)
10 seeds x 12 months `--curious`, with and without `--far-qi`: QR3/QR9 median day (+ seeds reaching QR9), Foundation day
(curious years), fights won/lost, injuries, artifact lives spent, stones at the end. Flag for the owner (F-005d) if
Foundation comes more than ~30% sooner. Under `Follow-ups:` say whether far regions' fights are fair for the player who
moved (count fights rated < 40% after the move).
