# GFX-216: A title worth stopping at (world-ui)

Reference: Eastward's and Sea of Stars' animated pixel title scenes (look only: layered parallax, one slow living
detail). Mechanics/look only, no names, art or story.

## Why (planner shot 2026-11-20 00:10, `screenshot_screens.gd -- --title --creation`, 1280x800)
The title is the first thing a player sees and was last touched in GFX-105. In the shot:
1. The three "clouds" (`TitleBackdrop.cloud_strips`, drawn in `_draw`) are flat 2-px rectangles with hard ends. At the left
   edge they read as glitch bars sticking out from behind the menu plaque (y ~240 and ~440 on screen).
2. The pine silhouettes of `_paint_pines` (i = 0..2) stand right behind the semi-transparent menu buttons: tree tips show
   through "Credits" and "Quit".
3. The robed figure on the rock (`_paint_rock_and_figure`, fx 158) is 3x9 px: at 4x it is a pale smudge at the bottom
   centre that nobody reads as a person.
4. Nothing on screen says "cultivation": no qi, no light in the pavilion beyond one pixel.

## Do
All in `src/ui/title_backdrop.gd` (pure static painters + `_draw`/`_process`) and, for (2), `src/ui/main_menu.gd`.
No binary assets; keep `SEED`-driven RNG; keep `reduced_motion()` freezing everything that moves.

1. **Soft clouds.** Replace the flat strips with pixel cloud shapes: each cloud is 2-3 overlapping rounded lobes
   (rows of decreasing width, top row narrowest, 1-px darker underside row), alpha 0.30-0.45, painted once into a small
   `Image` per cloud (static `cloud_image(index) -> Image`, deterministic) and drawn with `draw_texture_rect` at an integer
   pixel x. They wrap smoothly across the whole 320 px width (enter off one edge, leave off the other; never clipped to x=0).
   Keep 3 clouds at the current speeds.
2. **Menu stands on clear ground.** Either skip pines whose x falls inside the menu column (the plaque + button VBox in
   main_menu.gd spans backdrop x ~10-102 at 1280x800; compute it from `canvas_origin`/`pixel_scale` or simply keep pines at
   x >= 110), or give the button column an opaque `UIStyle.panel_style()` panel. Prefer moving the pines: the
   scene should stay visible. Add a static `pine_xs() -> Array[int]` so a test can check it.
3. **A cultivator you can see.** Redraw the figure seated in lotus pose on the rock, about 7 px wide x 10 px tall in the
   320x200 canvas (head, hair bun, robe with a 1-px darker fold, crossed legs as a wider base), facing the pavilion.
   Around it, 4-6 pale cyan-white qi motes (reuse the GFX-215 colour; you may call `QiMotes` helpers if they fit) rise
   slowly from the figure, 1 px each, fading out over ~3 s, drawn in `_draw` from a fixed pool (no allocation per frame).
   Under `reduced_motion()` draw them at fixed positions.
4. **The pavilion breathes light.** Its window pixel gets a 3x3 warm glow (alpha pulse 0.25-0.6 over 4 s); one or two
   tiny lantern dots under the eaves. Frozen under reduced motion.
5. **Optional, only if under budget:** a 1-px shooting star every 9-14 s (seeded) crossing the upper sky in 0.5 s.

## Tests (tests/unit/test_title_backdrop.gd, extend)
- `cloud_image(i)` is deterministic (same bytes twice) and has no fully opaque pixel; every cloud's drawn x range covers
  positions < 0 and > W over one wrap (never pinned at 0).
- `pine_xs()` has no x in the menu column (< 110).
- `build_scene()` still returns 320x200 and is deterministic.
- The figure's bounding box (non-sky pixels in the rock area) is at least 7x10.
- With reduced motion, two `_process` steps leave the mote positions unchanged.

## Acceptance
Render `screenshot_screens.gd -- --title --creation` at 1280x800 and 1280x720 and open both PNGs: no hard-edged bars,
nothing pokes through the buttons, the seated figure and its motes read at a glance, creation's panel still covers the
scene cleanly. Paste a one-line description of each shot in the commit message. `tools/test.sh`.
