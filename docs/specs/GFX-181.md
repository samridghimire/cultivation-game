# GFX-181: A meadow with life in it

**Role:** world-ui. **Reference:** Stardew Valley's farm and town grass (look only: tufts, clumps, worn yards with soft edges). Never copy its art.

## Problem (planner shot 2026-11-17 06:10, Qingshi autumn, `hud_arrival.png` at 1280x800)
The Qingshi ground (style `grass`) reads as one flat green sheet at play zoom: the 16 px variants, patch shading and clover
(GFX-130) are too fine to see. The worn yards (`_apply_worn`, e.g. around the Temple of Mercy, the bounty board, Farmer Tian's
plot) are smooth brown ellipses that look like stains, not trodden earth. The ridge got this treatment in GFX-175
(`_alpine_life`); the village never did.

## Build (src/world/ground_tiles.gd only, plus its caller's `life` dict if needed)
1. `static func meadow_blobs(rid: String, kind: String, count: int, size_px: Vector2i, avoid: Array) -> Array[Vector3]`:
   like `alpine_blobs` (pure hashes, same `rid` -> same result) but drops any blob whose centre lies in an `avoid` rect
   (the same `life["avoid"]` rects clover already avoids: places, roads). Pure, tested.
2. `static func _meadow_life(img, rid, season, avoid)` called from `ground_texture` for `style == "grass"` (after
   `_apply_worn`, before `_apply_season`):
   - **Tuft clumps:** ~10 clumps per 1000x1000 px, each 4-9 tufts scattered within ~14 px; a tuft is 3-5 px tall,
     2 tones (base darkened 0.18 at the root, base lightened 0.12 at the tip), so they read at 1x zoom.
   - **Lone tufts:** ~60 per 1000x1000 px, 2-3 px.
   - **Season detail:** Spring: white/pale-yellow clover blossoms in 1/3 of the clumps. Summer: 1-2 px wildflowers
     (reuse `FLOWER_COLORS`) in 1/3 of the clumps. Autumn: tuft tips rust/straw (`a8602f`, `c9a55a`) and ~40 fallen
     leaves per 1000x1000 px (2 px, reds/oranges). Winter: tufts are drawn before the snow pass, then re-draw only the
     tips of 1/2 of them after `_apply_season` so dry stalks poke through the snow.
3. **Worn yards with a ragged edge:** in `_apply_worn`, perturb the ellipse distance with the existing shade grid more
   strongly near the rim (d in 0.75..1.0) so the edge breaks into lobes, add 2-4 px pebbles (path colour darkened/lightened)
   at a low density inside, and a fringe of tufts just outside the rim (a few per yard). Keep the centre where it is.
4. Draw-only and seeded; no rng from GameState. Keep the cache key valid (season is already in it). Build time: the
   whole Qingshi ground texture must not take more than ~30% longer than before (print `Time.get_ticks_msec` around one
   `ground_texture` call before/after in your run and put both numbers in the commit).

## Tests (tests/unit, extend the existing ground tiles test file: grep `alpine_blobs` in tests/unit)
- `meadow_blobs` is deterministic and never returns a centre inside an `avoid` rect.
- An Autumn Qingshi ground texture has more rust/straw-coloured pixels than its Summer one (sample count).
