# GFX-095: Places to explore that look different

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's map sites, where a ruined battlefield, a reed marsh and a forest path each
read at a glance (mechanics only: one look per site kind; all art is code-drawn here).

## Why
Planner shots 2026-11-07 (Qingshi, Misty Forest, Azure Peak, Withered Bone Marsh at 1280x800): every explore place
(Village Outskirts, Deep Woods, Herb Valley, Beast Ridge, Ancient Cave, Black Reed Shallows, Drowned Battlefield, ...) is the
same grey rock dome with a cave mouth (PixelPlace `_cave`, pixel_place.gd ~113). Ten identical domes make the regions
look samey and give no hint of what you will meet.

## Data
- New optional field on `type: explore` places in data/regions.json: `look`, one of `cave` (default, today's dome),
  `woods`, `ruins`, `reeds`, `ridge`, `trail`. Document it in regions.json `_doc` next to the place types and validate it
  in `GameData._validate()` (unknown value = load error naming the place).
- Set a look on all 10 explore places (grep `"type": "explore"`): e.g. Village Outskirts `trail`, Deep Woods `woods`,
  Herb Valley `woods`, Beast Ridge `ridge`, Sundered Peak Trail `ridge`, Drowned Battlefield `ruins`, Black Reed Shallows
  `reeds`, Ancient Cave and true caves `cave`. Use judgement for the rest.

## Drawing (src/world/pixel_place.gd, same text-row templates and palette as the other nature kinds)
Each look is a template the same size class as `_cave` (fits the place's `size`, drawn at Sprites.scale(), outlined):
- `woods`: a dark clump of 3-4 overlapping tree crowns (v, darker shade) with a narrow earth path (e) leading into a gap.
- `ruins`: two or three broken stone wall stubs (s/S) of different heights, a fallen pillar, a torn banner on a pole (r =
  the place colour).
- `reeds`: a small dark water patch with 8-12 reed stalks (v with a brown head) around it.
- `ridge`: two or three jagged grey crags (s/S) with a thin trail (e) winding between them.
- `trail`: a wooden trail marker post with a small sign board (t/T) beside a worn path patch and 2 shrubs.
- `cave`: unchanged.
Pass the look in through whatever path the explore site already uses to pick its art (explore_site.gd / place_art.gd
`draw` / PixelPlace `rows(kind, ...)`): simplest is a kind string like `explore_woods` that `rows()` maps, with `explore`
still meaning cave. Keep the `_draw()` fallback (place_art.gd `_cave`) for an unknown look. Keep each template's collision
/ interaction size unchanged.

## Tests
- GameData rejects an unknown `look` and accepts each of the six.
- `PixelPlace.rows("explore_<look>")` (or your equivalent) is non-empty and rectangular for every look; `cave` matches today's.
- Every explore place in data/regions.json has a valid look (a data test).
