# C-380: a first lesson within a month

Role: content (data/*.json; a tiny hook only if an effect key is missing). Reference: Stardew Valley's friendship
(small daily kindnesses add up) and Tale of Immortal's teachers who take on a willing student (mechanics only).

## Problem
Qingshi's Next steps tells a newcomer "No fighting style: Hunter Ma teaches Iron Fist", and C-362 made his lesson free
once his favor reaches 15. QA-224's sim (gifts liked items, asks at the favor need) learns it on median day 104, about
60 days later than a player who just buys the manual (~day 45). The friendly path should be the quicker or equal one.
Named villagers can't be chatted with (`Family.check_chat` refuses them; they use dialogue), so favor comes only from
gifts, dialogue and his weekly choice (C-371).

## Change
1. Read where Hunter Ma's favor can rise before graduation: his dialogue tree (data/npcs.json / dialogue file, grep
   `hunter_ma`), gift tastes, C-371's weekly choice, encounters with `villager_favor`. List them in the commit with
   the favor each gives.
2. Add one early, honest source, available from day 1 to a player who has not graduated with him:
   a Hunter Ma deed (data/deeds.json, context `hunter_ma_errand` or a new one his place already offers; check how
   `ma_trap_raider` is wired) "Help Hunter Ma check his snare line" (1-2 days, `once: true`, its own `blocked_by_flag`,
   no fight, reward a little venison/boar meat and favor with him: use the effect key encounters use for a villager
   (`villager_favor`) if deeds go through `Effects.apply`, else add the smallest hook in the deed system with a test).
   Favor so that one errand + two liked gifts reaches 15. A second, repeatable-weekly variant is out of scope.
3. Make sure the newcomer sees it: Hunter Ma's menu lists the errand (check `Deeds.available` with his context) and
   the "No fighting style" hint still names him (no hint change needed if it already does).
4. Text: errand name and result line in his voice (terse, "Hm."), each <= 140 chars.

## Check
- tests: a unit test that a fresh Qingshi character can take the errand on day 0, and that errand + 2 liked gifts
  reaches favor >= 15 (use the gift-favor numbers from data); `test_data_references.gd` passes.
- Sim: `simulate_curious_years.gd -- 10 12 --chat-teacher` (QA-224's policy; teach it to take the errand if it only
  gifts, a few lines in tests/sim/first_hour.gd) and paste the first-style day per seed before/after. Target median
  <= 45 days. Balance values change: run `tools/balance.sh --check` and refresh the sections it explains.
