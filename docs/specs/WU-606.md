# WU-606: A lecture you sit through

Role: world-ui. Reference: Tale of Immortal's sect lectures (mechanics only: a monthly event at the hall that shows what
the elder taught and what you gained). Look: Wandering Sword's small staged vignettes (look only, our own drawing).
Size: ~300 lines with tests.

## Why
`GameState.attend_lecture()` (game_state.gd ~3580) posts the elder's scene line and "+N qi" to the message log, then a
2-day time skip. The best-written sect content (C-270's nine lines per elder) scrolls by in the log; nothing marks the
monthly lecture as an event.

## Build
1. Signal (GameState only, core stays signal-free): add to src/autoload/event_bus.gd
   `signal sect_lecture_attended(sect_id: String, result: Dictionary)` (doc comment: emitted after the lecture's time
   passes; `result` is `Sects.attend_lecture`'s dict plus `insight_name`). In `attend_lecture()` emit it after
   `_pass_time(...)`, with `result.duplicate()` and `"insight_name"` filled from `Dao.def_of` ("" when none). Keep the log
   lines (the log stays the record).
2. HUD: `_on_sect_lecture_attended` appends `{"kind": "sect_lecture", "sect_id", "result"}` to `_pending_moments` and calls
   `_update_modal()` (same queue as WU-604/WU-603, so it waits for the time-skip card and any open menu). Add the match arm
   next to "sect_promoted" (~1935).
3. SectCard (src/ui/sect_card.gd): `open_lecture(sect_name: String, lecture_name: String, line: String, qi: int,
   insight_name: String, color: Color)`:
   - title row: banner + sect name (as the other modes), subtitle = lecture name (capitalised first letter);
   - a drawn vignette 240x72 px (inner class `LectureScene extends Control`, `_draw()` only): a seated elder figure on a
     dais at the centre in the sect colour (darker robe), 4-6 seated disciples in two rows facing the elder (PixelFigure
     looks if they can be drawn seated; otherwise simple 3-4 rect seated silhouettes at Sprites.scale()), a hanging scroll
     or incense burner either side. Static (no animation needed), crisp at integer scale, nothing outside its rect.
   - the scene line in quotes-free body text, wrapped (the same label setup `open_report` uses for the steward line);
   - result rows: "+N qi" (or "Your qi is at a bottleneck: the words settle in your mind" when qi == 0) and, when
     `insight_name != ""`, a highlighted row "Insight glimpsed: <name>" in UIStyle's accent/warning colour;
   - a footer row "Next lecture: next month" (dim).
   - Continue button focused (grab_focus), same close rules (ui_accept / ui_cancel) as the other modes.
4. Shot flag in tests/sim/screenshot_first_hour.gd: `--sect-lecture=<sect_id>` (join the sect, mark reported, set the
   current region to its home, call `GameState.attend_lecture()`, let the queue play) -> `sect_lecture.png`. Add a
   `--insight` modifier that forces the insight (set the result's insight_name directly if the rng makes it awkward).
   Document both in the header comment.

## Edge cases
- Rogue by the time the moment plays (left the sect in between): skip, as `_play_sect_promoted` does.
- Lecture without `lines`: line = "You attend <name>." (what GameState posts).
- Reduced motion: nothing animates anyway.

## Tests (tests/unit/test_sect_cards.gd or the file WU-603/WU-604 used; grep `open_report` in tests/unit)
- `open_lecture` text (all_text()) contains the lecture name, the line and "+12 qi"; with qi 0 the bottleneck row; with an
  insight the "Insight glimpsed" row; without, none.
- attend_lecture as a member at home emits `sect_lecture_attended` once with qi and insight_name keys (test_game_session.gd
  style integration test); a refused lecture (already attended) emits nothing.
- The HUD queues the lecture moment while another modal is open (copy WU-603's `test_promotion_waits_for_open_menu`).
