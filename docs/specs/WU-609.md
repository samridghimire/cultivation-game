# WU-609: The promotion trial, staged

Role: world-ui. Reference: Tale of Immortal's sect promotion exams (mechanics only: you see who examines you and your
odds, then choose to step in); Wandering Sword's duel staging (look only). Size: ~300 lines with tests.

## Why
The trial for the first rank (Azure Cloud Sword Examiner, Blood Pit Gladiator, Pavilion Vault Guard) is the one fight
that decides a disciple's rise, but today it is a plain row "Attempt promotion trial" (sect_hall.gd ~130, mission_board.gd
~636) that jumps straight into `GameState.attempt_promotion_trial()`. The promotion card (WU-603) celebrates afterwards,
but nothing builds up to it, and the player never sees their odds before committing.

## Design
New `src/ui/trial_card.gd` (class `TrialCard`, a HUD screen registered with `hud._add_screen`, open()/close()/`closed`):
- Header: the sect banner (reuse SectCard's banner drawing) and "Trial for <rank name>".
- A drawn arena vignette (code-drawn, ~240x80, nearest filter, sect colour floor stripe): the player's PixelFigure look
  on the left, the examiner on the right (the enemy's look if it has one, else a robed figure in the sect colour),
  facing each other; reuse WU-606's LectureScene/vignette helpers if they fit.
- Lines: "<Examiner name> (<realm/stage>)"; "Your odds: <Combat.odds_word> (N in 100)" coloured like the combat
  report's odds line (WU-584 wording); "A sparring match: losing can injure you, but never kills or robs you."; and
  what the rank brings (SECT-034's `promotion_summary` perks for the next rank).
- Buttons: "Step into the arena" (calls `GameState.attempt_promotion_trial()` after closing) and "Not yet" (closes).
  Focus starts on "Not yet" when odds < 30 in 100, else on "Step into the arena"; B/Esc closes.
- Both entry points (sect hall row and mission board row) open the card instead of fighting directly. When
  `Sects.check_promotion` returns a reason, keep the disabled row with its reason as today (no card).
- Odds: compute once on open with `Combat.win_chance(c, data, enemy)` (the same enemy dict
  `Sects.trial_opponent(data, Sects.trial_enemy(c, data))` returns); keep it off the frame loop.
- Shot flag `--sect-trial=<sect_id>` in tests/sim/screenshot_first_hour.gd: join, report, set contribution to the rank-1
  need (no trial), open the card (`sect_trial.png`). Render it at 1280x800 and 1280x720 and open the PNGs.

## Tests (tests/unit/test_trial_card.gd)
- The card shows the examiner's name, the rank name and an odds line with "in 100".
- "Not yet" closes without a fight (player stats, sect rank and day unchanged); "Step into the arena" calls the trial
  (stub or check the day/log moved and the card closed).
- The hall's trial row opens the card when promotion is possible, and stays disabled with the reason otherwise.
- Focus: some control has focus after open (gamepad).
