# GFX-082: Ground without seams, a clear gate

Role: world-ui. Reference: Eastward / Stardew Valley ground (texture varies without visible tile edges), mechanics only.

## Why
Planner shots 2026-11-06 12:10 (`screenshot_first_hour.gd -- --great --region=qingshi_village,azure_peak,misty_forest`,
1280x800, files region_*.png):
- Azure Peak's ground shows a grid of darker and lighter **rectangles** (about 32-64 px, hard straight edges, a vertical band
  left of the river at x~160-200 and blocks around the hall): the ground-variant cells (`GroundTiles.variant_for` /
  `make_variant`, src/world/ground_tiles.gd ~46-90) differ in brightness and meet with hard edges. Misty Forest shows the same
  faintly. It reads as a broken texture, not a mountain.
- A pine tree stands on Azure Peak's region gate / name board (the "Azure Peak" sign at x~250, y~390, a tree drawn over the
  post). GFX-076 cleared trees off the Qingshi gate; the decor scatter still lands on other regions' gates.

## Spec
1. In GroundTiles, make variants differ only in speckle placement and in small colour jitter (luminance difference between
   any two variants of a region <= 3% of the base), or cross-fade a cell's edge 8-12 px into its neighbour; pick whichever
   is simpler in the existing code. The scree/grass/bog/litter styles keep their look; caching (`cached`) stays.
2. Decor (trees, rocks, clusters: the Scenery scatter) stays at least 40 px from every `travel` place and region gate/name
   board footprint in every region (find how GFX-076 kept Qingshi's gate clear and apply it to all gates; one shared rule,
   not per-region data).
3. Unit tests: (a) for a fixed region id and base colour, the mean luminance of every variant is within 3% of the others;
   (b) for every region in data/regions.json, no decor tree/rock position lies within 40 px of a travel place or the gate rect
   (use the Scenery layout function the world builder calls, no scene needed).
