# GFX-114: Winter that looks like winter

Role: world-ui. Reference: Stardew Valley's winter maps (mechanics only: what changes on screen when snow falls; no art,
names or layouts). Size: one run, under ~400 changed lines. Code-drawn, no binary assets.

## What the planner saw (2026-11-10, `--region=qingshi_village,withered_bone_marsh,misty_forest --season=Winter`, 1280x720)
Snowy ground, bare trees, frozen ponds and snowy roads already read as winter. What breaks it:
1. **Grey discs under every snowy pine.** `world.gd _draw_tree` (~1708) draws a 20 px round shadow at alpha 0.25 for
   every tree that is not bare. On snow that is a flat grey coin under each pine (Misty Forest has ~40 of them).
2. **Summer crowns in winter.** Explore places with the woods look (PixelPlace / PlaceLooks woods, e.g. "Deep Woods",
   "Herb Valley" in Misty Forest) keep dark summer-green round crowns with no snow.
3. **A green herb patch in deep snow.** Gather sites (GFX-102 looks: patch, terrace, bog ...) draw green sprouts all
   year ("Herb Patch" in Misty Forest).
4. **Jetties beside frozen ponds.** "Misty Pond Jetty" is a small crate next to the ice and "Bog Pond Shore"'s planks
   start below the pond. In winter a frozen pond should show where you fish: an ice hole.

## Build
1. Tree shadows (`_draw_tree`): when the ground season is Winter, every tree (pine, round, willow, bamboo) uses a foot
   ellipse like `_draw_foot_shadow`, coloured from the snow shade (`Color("9fb0c2")`, alpha ~0.35), not the round blot.
   Other seasons unchanged.
2. Woods/explore crowns: wherever the woods explore template draws crowns, in Winter draw a 2-3 px snow cap on the top
   of each crown (`e8eef4` with a `c8d4e0` underside line) and darken/desaturate the crown green by ~25%. Spring/Summer/
   Autumn unchanged (Autumn may keep its own tint if one exists).
3. Gather sites in Winter: green sprouts become dormant stubs (2 px brown/grey stalks) with a few snow pixels, for the
   patch, terrace and bog looks; rubble/crystal/frost/crater looks unchanged. A region with `map.no_snow` keeps its
   normal look (follow `GroundTiles.ground_season`).
4. Ice hole: when a pond draws frozen (`PixelLandmark.frozen_pond`, GFX-043), draw a dark-blue 10x6 ellipse "ice hole"
   with a white rim at the jetty's water end (Scenery.jetty_layout gives the jetty geometry; for a pond with no jetty
   drawn, at the pond point nearest the fishing place). Also check both jetty layouts against the pond rect in winter:
   the planks must start on the shore and end on the ice, as in other seasons (if a region's jetty data is off, fix the
   data in regions.json, not the drawing).

## Tests
- A pure helper for the shadow choice, e.g. `Scenery.tree_shadow(season: String, bare: bool) -> String` returning
  "foot" or "round"; unit test: Winter -> "foot" for every kind, Summer non-bare -> "round".
- A unit test for the ice-hole point: for every region with a fishing place and a pond, the point lies inside the pond
  rect.
- Keep existing tests green (`tools/test.sh`).

## Acceptance
Render `--region=qingshi_village,withered_bone_marsh,misty_forest --season=Winter` and the same in Summer at 1280x720,
open all six PNGs: no grey discs under pines, snowy crowns on woods, dormant herb patch, an ice hole where you fish;
summer shots unchanged. Say what you saw in the commit message.
