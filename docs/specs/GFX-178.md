# GFX-178: Streams that wander

Role: world-ui. Reference: Stardew Valley's forest river and Wandering Sword's map streams (look only: a stream bends,
widens and narrows, has banks and stones; never their art).

## Problem
Planner shots 2026-11-16 18:10 (`--region=misty_forest` Summer and Winter, `--region=azure_peak` Winter, 1280x800): the
stream in Misty Forest, Azure Peak and Myriad Peaks Ridge is a ruler-straight 30 px band from the top of the map to the
bottom. GFX-135 bends it with `Scenery.STREAM_AMP = 4.0` (scenery.gd ~2442), cropped inside the 30 px texture
(`PixelLandmark.meander`, pixel_landmark.gd ~62), so the bend is invisible. Frozen streams (GFX-116) are just as straight.

## What to build
1. **A real meander.** Raise the amplitude to about 36-48 px (pick one const, e.g. `STREAM_AMP := 40.0`) and the wavelength
   to 320-520 px (seeded per stream), add a second smaller seeded harmonic so it isn't a pure sine. Keep `stream_offset`'s
   rule that the offset is exactly 0 within 24 px of a path crossing (bridges stay straight) and eases in over the next
   ~60 px (was 24).
2. **Width that breathes.** The bed width varies 24-38 px along the length (seeded), never under 22 px at a bridge.
3. **Drawn in world space.** The 30 px texture can no longer hold the bend. Either widen the stream's texture to
   `thickness + 2 * STREAM_AMP` (and its draw rect) and keep `meander()`, or draw the bed as polygons along the centre
   line; pick the smaller change. Bed: water colour as today, a 3-4 px muddy/sandy bank band each side (darker on the
   shaded bank), a few pebbles and 1-2 riffles (short white dashes) per screen, reed tufts on inner bends in
   spring/summer/autumn (seeded). Keep GFX-047's downstream scroll and `MotionFx.reduced()` static mode.
4. **Frozen too.** Winter (`frozen`) follows the same centre line and width: pale ice with a few crack lines, snow on the banks.
5. **Nothing stands in the water.** The bed now leaves the landmark's 30 px rect. Wherever the keepouts use the stream
   landmark's rect (scenery.gd ~901, ~1311, ~2273, ~3080; `Scenery.landmark_rects`), grow a stream's rect sideways by
   `STREAM_AMP` so trees, decor, place art and NPC spots keep clear. If a place, jetty landmark, NPC spot or the arrival
   ring sits within `STREAM_AMP + 16` px of the stream's axis, ease the offset to 0 near it (like a path crossing) instead
   of moving content in data/regions.json. Azure Peak's Cloud-Stream Ford and the ridge jetty (pos [510, 704]) must still
   reach the water.

## Tests (tests/unit, e.g. test_scenery.gd)
- `stream_offset` is 0 within 24 px of a span, reaches >= 0.8 * STREAM_AMP somewhere on a 1000 px stream, and stays
  <= STREAM_AMP everywhere.
- For every region in data/regions.json with a `stream` landmark: no place rect, NPC spot, jetty landmark (beyond its
  water end) or arrival ring intersects the bed (sample the centre line every 8 px with the local half-width).
- Deterministic: the same region gives the same centre line twice.

## Acceptance
- Render `--region=misty_forest,azure_peak,myriad_peaks_ridge` with `--season=Summer` and `--season=Winter` at 1280x800,
  open them: the stream visibly bends at least once per screen, bridges meet straight water, nothing sits in the water,
  jetties reach it. Draw-only: no balance check. `tools/test.sh` passes.
