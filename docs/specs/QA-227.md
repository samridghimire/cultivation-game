# QA-227: Does a diligent disciple meet the monthly duty?

Role: systems (sim code). Reference: Tale of Immortal's sect contribution and chores (mechanics only).

## Why
QA-174 (a4ea1c1): with `--mission-monthly` the sect sim's rank-1 months still end 0-14 of 30 contribution short, and
151-154 of 370-450 sect months skip the stipend. But SECT-006 gave every home hall a steward with two repeatable chores
(1 day: 5 contribution; 2 days: 12 contribution; up to ~98 a month, counted toward the duty), and `Guidance.duty_hint`
names the steward chore (guidance.gd ~3499 `_steward_chore_line`). tests/sim/first_hour.gd never does steward chores
(its `CHORES` are the Qingshi headman deeds), so the sim cannot tell us whether a player who follows the hint meets the duty.

## What to build (tests/sim/first_hour.gd, sim only; base output unchanged)
1. `--steward-chores` (part of the `play_cached` memo key): for a sect member in a region with their own sect's hall,
   when `Sects.duty_progress(c) < Sects.monthly_duty(c, data)` and `Sects.duty_days_left(c)` <= 10, do the steward
   chore the duty hint names through the same GameState action the steward menu uses (grep `steward` in
   src/autoload/game_state.gd and src/world) until the duty is met or the chore is on cooldown. If the member is not at
   the hall, travel there only when the trip is <= 3 route days (GameState travel).
2. Extend `--sect-report` with two per-seed columns QA-174 could not give: the day the player became Inner Disciple
   (or "-") and the Foundation day (or "-"), plus the medians.
3. Run `simulate_curious_years.gd -- 10 36 --sect-report` for: base, `--mission-monthly`, `--steward-chores`, and both.
   Paste the four rows (stipends paid / skipped, missions, chores done, months short, Inner Disciple median day,
   Foundation median day) in the commit.

## Answer in the commit
- Does a disciple who follows the hint (chores, with or without one mission) meet the duty in >= 80% of months?
  If yes: the duty is fine and the gap was the sim. If no: name what is short (chore cooldowns? hall far from the qi
  spot?) under `Follow-ups:` for content/systems; do not retune data/sects.json here.
- What chores cost in meditation days (Foundation median day with vs without).
