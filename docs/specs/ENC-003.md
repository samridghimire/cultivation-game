# ENC-003: Familiar sights fade (fresh encounters)

**Why.** QA-041: from month 7 on the curious player's days are explore + sect mission. 200 encounters exist, but the
unflagged, repeatable non-fight ones (forest_herbs, forest_lost, ...) come back again and again at the same weight, so a
region feels "done" quickly while rarer encounters stay rare. Make encounters you have already met a lot less likely, and
give that weight to the ones you have not met yet, without changing how often you fight.

## Rules
- New `CharacterData.encounter_counts: Dictionary` (encounter id -> times met, int). `to_dict`/`from_dict` with default `{}`
  (old saves start fresh; no SAVE_VERSION bump needed, but add the key to the round-trip test).
- `GameState._explore_once` (src/autoload/game_state.gd ~575): after an encounter is chosen (rolled or discovery), call
  `Exploration.note_met(player, encounter)` which increments `encounter_counts[id]`.
- An encounter **fades** when all hold: no `enemy`, no `blocked_by_flag` (one-shots already disappear), not
  `only_if_applicable`, not `discovery_only`, and not `"repeatable": true` (new optional key; document it in
  data/encounters.json `_doc`, validate it is a bool in GameData's encounter validation).
- In `Exploration.eligible_encounters` (src/core/systems/exploration.gd ~136), after the existing weight math:
  `weight *= freshness(c, e)` where
  ```gdscript
  const FRESHNESS_DECAY := 0.5
  const FRESHNESS_FLOOR := 0.2
  static func freshness(c: CharacterData, e: Dictionary) -> float  # 1.0 if it doesn't fade, else max(FLOOR, 1 / (1 + DECAY * count))
  ```
- **Keep the fight share.** Before returning, rescale the fading entries so the total weight of all non-fight entries is the
  same as it would have been without fading: `k = sum(non-fight weights before fading) / sum(non-fight weights after)`;
  multiply every non-fight entry (fading or not) by `k`. Fight entries are untouched. (Net effect: within the non-fight
  share, fresh encounters get more of it.) Guard the division when the sum is 0.
- `outlook` uses `eligible_encounters`, so it follows automatically; check its shares still sum to 1.
- Mark these `"repeatable": true` in data/encounters.json (they are the region's bread and butter, not "sights"):
  forest_herbs and any other encounter whose only effect is a herb/material `items` gain with no text-story (list the ids
  you marked in the commit; keep it to the plain gathering ones, at most ~10).

## Tests (tests/unit/test_exploration.gd or a new test_encounter_freshness.gd)
- `freshness` is 1.0 for a fight, a flagged one-shot, only_if_applicable, repeatable; 1.0 / 0.667 / 0.5 / ... / 0.2 floor
  for a plain fortune met 0/1/2/.../many times.
- With a fixed tag set, the summed weight of fight entries is identical with counts {} and with a big count on every
  fading encounter; the non-fight total is identical too (float tolerance).
- After meeting encounter A ten times, A's share of 2000 seeded rolls drops by at least half versus fresh, and another
  non-fight encounter's share rises.
- `encounter_counts` round-trips through to_dict/from_dict; a dict without the key loads as {}.
- GameState integration (test_game_session.gd): exploring once in misty_forest increments the met encounter's count.

## Acceptance
`tools/test.sh` passes. Run `tests/sim/simulate_economy.gd` before and after and paste the explore income lines; they
must stay within ~10% (fortunes keep their share). Under ~250 changed lines.
