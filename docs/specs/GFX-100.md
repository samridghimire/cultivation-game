# GFX-100: Every region builds its own way

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's towns, where each region's houses share a roof shape, wall colour and trim
that tell you where you are at a glance (mechanics/presentation idea only; no names, layouts or art).

## Why
Planner shots 2026-11-08 06:10 (`--region=withered_bone_marsh,fallen_star_market,azure_peak` and Qingshi): every
building kind (workshop, clinic, family home, house landmark, shop) uses one palette (`PixelPlace.COLORS`: cream walls
`w`/`W`, brown timber `t`/`T`) in every region. The marsh's Bonefire Workbench is the same cream cottage as Qingshi's
Village Workshop; Azure Peak's Hermit Forge too. Buildings also sit on the ground with no contact shadow, so they look
pasted on.

## What to build
1. **`map.building_style`** (optional string) in data/regions.json, one of `thatch` (default, today's look), `tile`
   (market: grey-blue tiles, white walls, red pillars), `stone` (Azure Peak / Myriad Peaks: grey stone walls `s`/`S`,
   slate-blue roof), `stilt` (Withered Bone Marsh: dark weathered wood walls, reed-brown roof, the base raised on 3-4 px
   posts over a darker mud strip), `log` (Misty Forest: dark log walls with horizontal 1 px seams, moss-green roof edge).
   Document it in regions.json `_doc` and validate it in `GameData._validate()` (unknown style = load error). Set it for
   every region that has building places (keep Qingshi `thatch`).
2. **Style palettes, not new templates.** In `src/world/pixel_place.gd` add `const STYLE_COLORS := {style: {char: Color}}`
   that overrides `COLORS` entries (`w`,`W`,`t`,`T`,`e` and the roof colour passed as `color`) and pass the style through
   `texture(kind, size, color, active, place_name, style := "thatch")` into the cache key and `from_rows`. Only building
   kinds take a style (`workshop`, `clinic`, `orphanage`, `family_home`, `auction`, `shop*`, `merchant`); sect halls,
   nature kinds, explore/realm looks are unchanged. `stilt` and `log` add their small row edits in `rows()` (posts under the
   base, log seams) behind a `style` argument; keep each template edit under ~15 lines.
   `PixelLandmark` `house` kind (pixel_landmark.gd ~75) takes the same style for its walls/roof.
   The region's style reaches PixelPlace from world.gd where places and landmarks are built (`_region["map"]`).
3. **Contact shadow.** Every building-kind PixelPlace and `house` landmark draws a soft 2-step shadow under its base
   (an ellipse/rect 1.1x the footprint width, 6-8 px tall, `Color(0,0,0,0.22)` then `0.12` at the rim), drawn before the
   sprite, never on snow-capped roofs (it is at ground level, so it simply sits under the base).
4. Seasonal snow caps (GFX-037/043) still draw on every style's roof; winter tint unchanged.
5. Shot: add nothing new; `--region=qingshi_village,withered_bone_marsh,fallen_star_market,azure_peak,misty_forest`
   already renders every style. Look at all five at 1280x800 and say in the commit what you checked.

## Tests (tests/unit/test_pixel_place.gd or a new test_building_style.gd)
- Every style in `PixelPlace.STYLE_COLORS` produces a texture for each building kind, same size as the `thatch` one.
- Two styles give different textures (pixel at a wall position differs) and the cache keys differ.
- GameData rejects `building_style: "pagoda"` (copy a region dict in the test and validate it).
- Every regions.json region with a workshop/clinic/merchant place has a valid style or none.
- Default (no field) equals today's look: the `thatch` texture is byte-identical to the pre-change texture for a
  workshop (compare to `texture(kind, size, color)` with no style argument).
