# QA-016 First-hour guard sim and tests (qa)

## Goal
Lock in the first-hour fixes with a repeatable sim and guard tests, so later content can't silently break the newcomer path.

## Change
1. tests/sim/simulate_first_hour.gd (a SceneTree script like the other sims in tests/sim/): for N seeds (arg, default 30),
   start a new session (whatever GameState call character creation uses), then play a scripted newcomer in Qingshi:
   talk to Elder Mo, do Headman Zhou's chores (via the dialogue/deed calls the NPC menu uses), use every carried qi item,
   meditate at the Spirit Spring in 30-day steps, attempt the breakthrough when available, join the first sect that accepts,
   explore once per month. Print per seed: day of Qi Refining 1st/3rd/9th layer, day joined a sect, fights won/lost/fled,
   injuries suffered, artifact lives lost, message-log lines per month by category (EventBus history). Print the medians.
2. tests/unit/test_first_hour.gd (fast, a few seeds, seeded rng): guards
   - a newcomer who does the chores and uses the pills reaches Qi Refining 1st Layer within 30 days in at least 4 of 5 seeds;
   - no forced (non-lethal, not evaded) Qingshi encounter is below 15% win chance for a Qi Refining 1st-3rd layer player with
     the starter technique (iterate data.encounters with qingshi tags, Combat.win_chance with a crafted character), print the
     offenders in the failure message;
   - meditating 12 months in Qingshi posts at most ~2 world-news lines per month on average (after FH-005; until FH-005 lands,
     mark this check with a TODO and skip it).
3. Document the sim in docs/HANDOFF.md "Tools" is the planner's job; put a one-line usage comment at the top of the script.
