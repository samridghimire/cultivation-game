# WU-552: an encounter card with a name and the right place

Role: world-ui (+ a small data edit). Reference: Wandering Sword's event vignettes (look only).

## Problems (WU-550 follow-ups, planner shot 2026-11-16 encounter.png)
- Every ordinary encounter card is titled "Encounter" (`EncounterWindow.title_for`, src/ui/encounter_window.gd ~78-89).
- The spring swollen-ford encounter (`road_spring_swollen_ford`, scene "trail") draws a flowery meadow, not water.

## Change
1. Title. `title_for(encounter, discovery, deep_path)` keeps its discovery / hidden path / renowned forms. Otherwise:
   - if the encounter has a `name` field, use it;
   - else a title from the scene and the season: `SCENE_TITLES := {"trail": "On the road", "woods": "In the woods",
     "reeds": "In the reeds", "ruins": "Among the ruins", "ridge": "On the ridge", "cave": "In a cave",
     "village": "In the village", "river": "At the water"}`; an encounter with `seasons` gets the season first
     ("Spring, on the road"; lower-case the scene title after the comma). No scene: "On the road" outside a settlement,
     "In the village" inside one (the window already knows the region; settlements are regions whose map is a village
     look, use the same test encounter_window uses for `wild`).
   Keep it a pure static function.
2. A river scene. `PlaceLooks.EXPLORE_LOOKS` is also used for explore places, so add
   `const ENCOUNTER_SCENES := ["river"]` in src/core/systems/place_looks.gd and accept it in the GameData scene check
   (src/core/game_data.gd ~298, update the error message and the encounters.json `_doc` for `scene`).
   In src/ui/encounter_scene.gd add `"river": "river"` to `LOOK_BACKDROPS`, and draw the "river" backdrop in
   src/ui/duel_stage.gd next to the existing ones (~600-720): a far bank with grass/trees, a water band across the
   lower middle (season-tinted: brown and churning in spring, blue in summer, grey in autumn, ice edges in winter), a
   near bank of mud/stones where the figure stands, and props (reeds, a mooring post). Seeded/deterministic like the
   others; reuse the season colour helpers already there.
3. Data: set `"scene": "river"` on `road_spring_swollen_ford` and on other encounters whose text clearly happens at a
   river, ford, ferry, boat or jetty (`grep -n 'ford\|ferry\|river\|boat\|jetty' data/encounters.json`; at most ~15, list
   them in the commit). Don't touch encounters where water is only mentioned in passing.
