# GFX-098: Secret-realm entrances that look like where they lead

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's dungeon mouths, where a tomb door, a cave in a cliff and a sunken gate each read at a
glance (mechanics only: one look per entrance; all art is code-drawn here). Follows the pattern GFX-095 set for explore places.

## Why
Planner shots 2026-11-08 00:10 (`--region=qingshi_village,fallen_star_market,withered_bone_marsh,...`, 1280x800): every secret
realm entrance is the same ring (PixelPlace `_rift`, pixel_place.gd ~606), tinted by the place colour. In Qingshi the
"Blossom Crack in the Hill" is a flat pink donut in the middle of the grass, the "Crater Vault Seal" in Fallen Star Market is
a brown donut, and the "Sunken Palace Gate" in the marsh is a blue donut. They are the most mysterious places on each map and
they read as rugs.

## Data
- New optional field on `type: secret_realm` places in data/regions.json: `look`, one of `rift` (default, today's ring),
  `cleft`, `seal`, `gate`, `tomb`, `pavilion`. Document it in regions.json `_doc` next to GFX-095's explore `look` and
  validate it in `GameData._validate()` (unknown value = load error naming the place).
- Put the list of looks in a core-safe place: GFX-095 left `EXPLORE_LOOKS` in src/world/pixel_place.gd and GameData (core)
  reads it from there (reviewer note 2026-11-07). Move both lists into `PlaceLooks` (src/core, already used by GameData
  for deed-giver looks) as `PlaceLooks.EXPLORE_LOOKS` / `PlaceLooks.REALM_LOOKS`; PixelPlace refers to them.
- Set a look on all 6 entrances:
  | region | place | look |
  |---|---|---|
  | qingshi_village | Blossom Crack in the Hill | `cleft` |
  | misty_forest | Shimmering Rift | `rift` |
  | fallen_star_market | Crater Vault Seal | `seal` |
  | azure_peak | Sword-Scarred Rift | `cleft` |
  | withered_bone_marsh | Sunken Palace Gate | `gate` |
  | myriad_peaks_ridge | Skyfall Thunder Pavilion | `pavilion` |
  (`tomb` is for later realms; draw it anyway so content can use it.)

## Drawing (src/world/pixel_place.gd, same text-row templates and palette as the other nature kinds)
`world.gd` (~652) already maps explore looks to `art_kind` through `PixelPlace.explore_kind`; add `PixelPlace.realm_kind(look)`
the same way (`secret_realm` for `rift`, `secret_realm_<look>` otherwise) and handle the kinds in `rows()`. Keep `active`
(a realm is open, `SecretRealmEntrance.art_active()`): every look shows a closed state and a brighter open state.
Each template about the size of `_rift` (48x38), outlined:
- `cleft`: a grey-green hillock (s/S with a grass top) split by a dark jagged crack (d); closed: a faint glint (S) in the
  crack; open: the crack glows (h/p) and two or three petal or spark pixels drift out (place colour r).
- `seal`: a round stone lid set flush in a ring of rubble (s/S), carved with a cross of runes (r = place colour); open: the
  runes light up (h) and the lid shows a bright seam.
- `gate`: two stone pillars and a lintel half sunk in dark water (water tone like the marsh pond, or d), a dark doorway;
  open: a pale light (h/p) in the doorway and on the water.
- `tomb`: a low earth mound with a stone door and two small guardian stones; open: the door stands ajar with light.
- `pavilion`: a small tiered roof (roof helper, r/R) on four thin posts over a stone plinth; open: a bright orb (h/p)
  hangs under the roof.
- `rift`: unchanged.
The vector fallback `PlaceArt._rift` (place_art.gd ~269) only runs when the pixel template is missing; it may stay a rift for
every look.
