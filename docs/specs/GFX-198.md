# GFX-198: Ground glitches from the season pass

**Role:** world-ui. **Reference:** Wandering Sword's mountain and town maps (look only).

QA-235 rendered every region in every season after PERF-009 and found three glitches (681f7e6 Follow-ups):
1. **myriad_peaks_ridge, Spring and Summer:** a faint, regular dotted grid over the whole dark rock ground (most visible
   around x 200-900, y 450-700 at 1280x800). Autumn and Winter are clean, so it comes from a green-month pass:
   `_alpine_meadow` / `_alpine_life` (ground_tiles.gd ~590-646, GFX-191) or the PERF-009 grass noise on rock
   (`patch_step`/`_apply_patches` ~310-341 use 2x2 cells). Find it by toggling passes in a scratch copy of the shot,
   then break the regularity: jitter the cell lattice with `_ihash`, or drop the pass on `scree` style.
   Also Spring: a lone white blob at the far left edge (~x 25, y 510): clamp `alpine_blobs` centres to at least
   radius + 8 px inside the map, or skip blobs whose centre is within 24 px of an edge.
2. **fallen_star_market, Winter, Meteor Crater rim:** snow on the rim is blocky and blotchy (~x 530-750, y 420-580).
   The crater is a landmark (pixel_landmark.gd / world.gd landmark draw): give it its own winter look: a thin snow
   line on the upper rim (2-3 px, light) and a darker, snow-free inner bowl, instead of the ground's snow blocks
   showing through.
3. **fallen_star_market, Winter, paving:** snow patches on the paved zones end in hard rectangular edges. In
   `_winter_streets` (~785) / `winter_street_amount` (~754), fade snow over the last 4-6 px of a zone with a hashed
   dither (the GFX-193 2x2-cluster border is the house style), and leave a 2-px slush line along kerbs.
