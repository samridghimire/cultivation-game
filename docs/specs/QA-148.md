# QA-148: What does sect contribution buy? Spending it in the first year

## Why
QA-146 prints sect contribution: the 10-seed curious median is **1346 at month 12** (seed 67: 1730), and the sim never
spends any (`grep buy_with_contribution tests/sim/` finds nothing). Azure Cloud's shop (data/sects.json) sells, for
contribution: Qi Gathering Pill 15 (150 qi), **Superior Qi Gathering Pill 40 (400 qi)**, Manual: Stone Skin Tempering
45, Golden Bell Talisman 30, Meridian Mending Pill 100, and from Inner Disciple (rank 1) the **Foundation Establishment
Pill 250** and the Azure Cloud Heart Sutra 700. 1346 contribution of Superior pills is ~33 pills, ~13,000 qi: possibly
several layers. Before a hint points at it (GUIDE-135), measure it, because pacing is the owner's open question
(DESIGN.md F-005d) and QA-144 found stone-bought qi pills already bring QR9 ~13% sooner.

## Changes (sim only, no game code, no data)
1. `tests/sim/first_hour.gd`: `static var spend_contribution := false` (same style as `buy_qi`). When true, once a month
   after the existing actions, for a sect member, through `gs.buy_with_contribution(item_id)` (the call the mission
   board uses) and the Inventory's use-item GameState call:
   a. **Breakthrough pill:** in the last three stages of the realm (`Guidance._pill_goal_applies` or the same condition),
      if the sect shop sells the next realm's pill and c's rank allows it and the balance covers it, claim it (once).
      If GUIDE-132 is on main, use `Guidance.sect_pill_entry`.
   b. **Body art:** if the player knows no `body` technique (`Techniques.knows_kind(c, data, "body")`) and the shop sells
      a body manual the player can buy, buy and learn it (the use-item call learns a manual).
   c. **Qi:** reserve = the pill's contribution cost while (a) is still to come in this realm (rank 1 reachable or held,
      pill not yet claimed), else 0. Spend `balance - reserve` on the shop qi item with the most qi per contribution
      (`effects.qi > 0`) that `Sects.check_purchase` allows; use each pill and stop using at the bottleneck
      (`Cultivation.is_at_bottleneck`), keeping the rest (as QA-144 did with stones).
   Tally per seed: contribution spent on (a)/(b)/(c), pills used, qi gained, day the pill was claimed.
2. `tests/sim/simulate_first_hour.gd`: `--spend-contribution` sets it (add it to the argument filter). Print one summary
   line `Contribution spent (QA-148): pill N seeds (median day D), body art N, qi pills N for C contribution, qi Q`.
3. Default output (no flag) must stay byte-identical for `-- 10 12 --curious` (diff it).

## Report (commit message)
10 seeds x 12 months `--curious`, without and with `--spend-contribution` (and once with both `--spend-contribution
--buy-qi`):
- QR3 and QR9 median day (seeds reaching QR9), stage counts at m12, Foundation reached (seeds, median day);
- contribution at m12 (balance), Inner Disciple median day (spending must not delay the rank: rank uses total
  contribution; confirm);
- fights won/lost and injuries (the body art should cut injuries).
Under `Follow-ups:` say what GUIDE-135's hint should name (pill claim / body art / qi pills, and which reserve) and flag
it for the owner if QR9 comes more than ~30% sooner with qi pills from contribution. Don't change data or the default
policy; don't refresh the balance baseline (the flag is opt-in).
