# WU-492: A crafting window you can read

Role: world-ui. Reference: Stardew Valley's crafting tooltip and Immortal Life's alchemy furnace (mechanics only: an
ingredient row shows what you have, what you need and the nearest place to get it; the full list is one step away).

## Why (planner shots 2026-11-08 18:10, `screenshot_screens.gd` crafting.png)
The Alchemy window for a Foundation player runs off the bottom of a 1280x800 screen: under the ingredients
`missing_sources_text` (src/ui/crafting_screen.gd:131) prints every source of every missing ingredient as one grey
paragraph (Spirit Herb alone lists 15 places: "Sold at Village Herb Seller (Qingshi Village); Gathered at Old Quarry
Tailings (Qingshi Village); ...; Dropped by creatures; Reward from encounters; ..."). The Refine / Refine x5 buttons
are cut off at the bottom edge and the panel touches the top.

## Build
1. `CraftingScreen.nearest_sources(c, data, item_id, region_id, today, limit := 2) -> Array[String]` (static, pure
   on its inputs): from `Items.sources(data, item_id)`, keep the shop/gather lines (drop the generic "Dropped by
   creatures", "Reward from ..." lines), order them: this region first, then by route days from `region_id`
   (use the same route-days helper the shop's "(10 days)" note uses; grep `distance_note`), then name. A market-day
   stall keeps its "(market days 6 and 21)" note. Return at most `limit`.
2. `missing_sources_text` becomes one short line per missing ingredient:
   `Spirit Herb: Village Herb Seller (here), Old Quarry Tailings (here) +13 more`; when nothing is in reach (only the
   generic kinds), `Spirit Herb: dropped by creatures, found exploring`. Keep the WU-201 rule (gather sources the
   ingredient line already names are left out) and the existing callers.
3. The full list moves to the ingredient row's tooltip, and a focusable "All sources" toggle under the lines expands
   it inside a ScrollContainer (gamepad: the toggle is in the focus chain after the recipe list; ui_accept toggles).
4. Layout: the right-hand detail column is a ScrollContainer with the Refine / Refine x5 row pinned below it (outside
   the scroll), so the buttons are always on screen. The window keeps the HUD's top margin (not flush with y=0) at
   1280x800 and 1280x720.
5. Same treatment wherever `missing_sources_text` or a raw `Items.sources` join is shown in a crafting-like screen
   (grep: blacksmith/talisman/array share crafting_screen.gd; the cook menu if it lists sources).

## Tests (tests/unit/test_crafting_screen.gd or the existing crafting UI test)
- nearest_sources: here first, then by days; at most `limit`; generic lines dropped; the "+N more" count is right.
- missing_sources_text for the Qi Gathering Pill with an empty bag in Qingshi: one line per ingredient, each <= 90 chars.
- Layout test (await frames like test_focus_audit.gd): at 1280x720 the Refine button's global rect is inside the
  viewport and the window's top is >= 8 px.
