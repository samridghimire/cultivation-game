# GFX-149: Mountain trails, not highways

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's mountain paths and Stardew Valley's mountain trail (look only, never their art).

## Problem (planner shots 2026-11-15 00:10, 1280x720, `--region=azure_peak,myriad_peaks_ridge`)
Both scree regions (Azure Peak, Myriad Peaks Ridge; `map.ground_style: "scree"`) draw their main road as a 50-60 px band of
grey square flagstones in a regular grid that runs the full map width. It reads as a paved highway or a car park, not a path
up a mountain. The reason: `Scenery.path_tiles` (src/core/systems/scenery.gd ~812) treats `scree` exactly like `paved` (the
market town) and fills the whole road rect with slab rows.

## Change
1. In `Scenery.path_tiles`, split the `scree` branch from `paved` (keep `paved` byte-for-byte unchanged; the market must
   look the same).
2. Scree trail layout (all deterministic from the existing rng seed):
   - A packed-grit trail bed: `kind: "rut"` strips are not right here; add a new tile kind `"grit"` covering a wobbling
     strip 55-65% of the road width, centred, whose two edges wander by +-4 px every 24-40 px along the road (same idea as
     GFX-146's boardwalk wobble; reuse its helper if it has one).
   - Stepping flagstones only in the trail bed: irregular slabs 8-18 px long, 6-12 px wide, 1-2 abreast, with 4-14 px
     of grit between them; about 1 in 6 tilted (draw as a 4-point polygon, `kind: "flag_poly"` with a `points` array) and
     about 1 in 8 missing (just grit). Never a regular grid: no two consecutive slabs share both length and width.
   - Outside the trail bed, inside the road rect: scree/ground shows through (draw nothing, or `pebble` tiles at 1 per
     ~120 px^2), plus loose `pebble` clusters (2-4 pebbles) along both edges every 30-60 px.
   - Where a stream bridge rect overlaps (the existing `bridges` list), skip trail tiles as today.
   - Steps: where the road meets a `steps` landmark (Azure Peak's stair up to the hall), keep the steps as they are.
3. World draw (`World._draw_road`, src/world/world.gd ~2423): draw `grit` as the region ground lightened 0.12 and warmed
   slightly (lerp 0.15 toward "8a7a62"), `flag_poly` with `draw_colored_polygon` in `Scenery.paving_shade` and a 1 px
   darker outline on the lower/right edges only (a lit top, not a box). In winter (`snowy`), the grit bed shows packed snow
   ("dfe6ee"), slabs keep a 2 px snow cap on the top edge, and the off-trail part of the road rect is plain snow ground.
4. The scree road should no longer draw a full-rect verge or slab outline: the road's outer rectangle must be invisible
   (only the trail and pebbles show).

## Tests (tests/unit/test_road_look.gd)
- A scree region's `path_tiles` has no `slab` tile wider than 18 px and none outside the central 70% of the road width.
- At least one `grit` tile, at least one `flag_poly` tile, and the slab rows are not on a regular grid (the set of distinct
  slab start offsets across the road is > 3).
- A `paved` region (fallen_star_market) returns exactly the same tiles as before (compare tile count and the first 10 rects to
  a value captured on main before the change).
- Deterministic: two calls with the same region and rect return equal arrays.
