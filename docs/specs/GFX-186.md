# GFX-186: A forest you walk through

**Role:** world-ui. **Reference:** Wandering Sword's woodland maps (look only: dense canopy masses at the edges of a clearing, light patches where the path runs).

## Problem (planner shot 2026-11-17 12:10, `--region=misty_forest --season=Summer`, region_misty_forest.png)
1. The Misty Forest reads as a dim meadow with scattered single pines; nothing says "forest" except the colour.
2. Several full-height, straight-edged vertical bands of darker/lighter tint cross the map (e.g. around x 1040-1110 and
   near the left edge in the shot). Nothing on the map explains them; they look like a layer or ground-chunk seam.

## Build
1. **Find the bands first.** Toggle layers one at a time in a scratch run (Settings `ambient_effects` off, the
   `MistBands` node hidden, the ambient emitter off, the `litter` ground texture replaced by a flat colour) and render
   the same shot. Fix the cause where it lives (e.g. a ground texture whose chunks tile with different tints, a shade
   overlay drawn per column). Say in the commit what it was. If the cause is the `mist` particle preset
   (src/world/ambient.gd, 40 px square particles), make the particles soft round blobs.
2. **Canopy masses.** `data/regions.json` misty_forest `decor` has `trees: 120, groves: 7`. Add, in world.gd's decor pass,
   a new optional decor key `canopy_masses` (int; document it in regions.json `_doc` and validate it as 0..20 in
   `GameData._validate()`): that many big seeded clumps (6-14 overlapping trees, mostly pines and round trees from
   `tree_kinds`) placed away from places, paths, streams and ponds (reuse the avoid lists the groves use), each with one
   merged dark under-canopy shadow so the clump reads as one mass. Set `canopy_masses: 6` for misty_forest only.
3. **Undergrowth.** Inside and around each mass, a few ferns/bush blobs (season tinted; bare stalks in winter) so trunks
   do not float on flat ground.
4. Keep every place, NPC spot, label and the road clear: nothing from this pass may overlap a place footprint grown by 24 px.

## Tests
A unit test that the canopy-mass layout (put the pure placement in `Scenery` or `GroundTiles`, seeded by region id) is
deterministic and that no tree centre falls inside any place footprint + 24 px or any path rect, for misty_forest's data.

## Acceptance
Render `screenshot_first_hour.gd -- --region=misty_forest` for Summer and Winter at 1280x800 and open them: no vertical
bands, the forest reads as dense woods around clearings, labels and NPCs stay readable. Qingshi's shot is unchanged
(no `canopy_masses` there). tools/test.sh.
