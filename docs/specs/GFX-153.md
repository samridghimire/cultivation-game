# GFX-153: A ridge that isn't white bars and crates

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's mountain maps and Stardew Valley's mountain (look only, never their art).

## Problem (planner shot 2026-11-15 06:10, 1280x720, `--region=myriad_peaks_ridge`, Year 1 spring)
GFX-149 and GFX-150 landed, but at gameplay zoom in spring:
1. **Snow drifts read as white bars.** Bright white flat strips ~90 x 15 px sit in the open ground (left of the Thunder-Scorched
   Anvil label, under the Skyfall pavilion label, above the Ridge Hermit's Stall). They are the `drift` items from
   `Scenery.relief_layout` (src/core/systems/scenery.gd ~1355: a 30-90 x 10-16 rect under a ledge, `relief.snow` chance),
   drawn in src/world/world.gd `_draw_relief` ~1735 as a ragged white polygon. Summer only halves them; spring and autumn draw
   the full winter drift. A lone white bar in green-grey spring ground looks like a UI glitch, not snow.
2. **Some rock reads as grey crates.** Two blocks flank the Ridge Hermit's Stall (about 90 x 50 px each, flat lighter top strip,
   dark right edge, straight vertical sides). Find what draws them (ledge `_draw_rock_band`, `Scenery.high_crags`, or a
   PixelLandmark / place-art "cliff": add a temporary debug label or print rects to identify) and give them a rock silhouette.
3. **Stepping stones vanish on dark scree** (GFX-149 Follow-up): on Myriad's dark grey bed the stones are almost the bed's colour.
4. **Winter ridge never checked** (GFX-150 Follow-up): `--season=Winter --region=myriad_peaks_ridge` was not rendered.

## Changes
1. **Seasonal drifts.** Add a pure `Scenery.drift_look(season: String, seed_value: int) -> Dictionary` returning
   `{show: bool, scale: Vector2, color: Color, alpha: float, lumps: int}`:
   - Winter: as today (full size, snow white `e8eef4`, alpha 1, 1 lump = current ragged outline).
   - Autumn / Spring: a *shaded remnant* only: scale (0.45, 0.6), colour a cool pale grey-blue (`c9d3dc`), alpha 0.85, broken
     into 2-3 lumps (separate small ragged outlines along the original rect, gaps of 4-10 px) hugging the ledge's foot.
     Spring hides it entirely in 1 of 2 drifts (seeded), autumn in 2 of 3.
   - Summer: `show` false (no snow at all; the drift's slot stays empty).
   Use it in `_draw_relief`'s `"drift"` branch (season from `GroundTiles.ground_season`, already computed there). Regions with no
   relief snow are unaffected.
2. **Rock, not crates.** Whatever draws the two blocks: give it a polygon silhouette instead of a rect: an uneven top edge
   (3-5 steps or a `Scenery.jagged_outline` top, 4-10 px variation), sloped or rounded sides (top 6-12 px narrower than the
   base), a lit top-left facet and a dark lower-right face, 1-2 crack lines, and a soft `Scenery.shadow_blob` on the ground at
   its foot. If it is the ledge itself, raise `ledge_steps`' height spread so neighbouring steps differ by >= 25% and taper the
   two end steps to <= 50%. Pure helper changes get a unit test (vertex counts, end steps lower than the peak, deterministic).
3. **Stone contrast.** In `Scenery._scree_trail_tiles` / the scree branch of `path_tiles`, pick the stone colour from the bed:
   if the bed's luminance is below 0.45, lighten stones by >= 0.22 (and their lit top by a further 0.1); otherwise keep today's.
   Test: on Myriad's ground colour the stone vs bed luminance gap is >= 0.12; on Azure Peak's the old colours are unchanged.
4. **Winter check.** Render `--season=Winter --region=myriad_peaks_ridge` and fix only what is clearly broken (snow caps that
   miss the stepped ledge tops, white-on-white stepping stones on a snowy bed, drifts floating away from any rock).
