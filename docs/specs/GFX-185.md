# GFX-185: Dirt roads that are walked

**Role:** world-ui. **Reference:** Wandering Sword's village roads (look only: worn earth with soft verges, ruts and tufts, never a ruled band).

## Problem (planner shots 2026-11-17 12:10, Qingshi Spring hud_arrival.png and Misty Forest Summer region shot)
On `ground_style: "grass"` (Qingshi) and `"litter"` (Misty Forest) maps the main road is a flat brown band with two
ruler-straight edges, and Qingshi's side lanes (e.g. the lane south of the Temple of Mercy, the lane east of the main
road near the bottom) are thin straight strips that stop dead. Scree, bog and moss roads already got wandering edges
(GFX-164/167/172, GFX-156, GFX-170); the dirt road a new player walks first never did.

## Build
1. `src/core/systems/scenery.gd`: add a pure, seeded helper
   `static func dirt_road(rect: Rect2, seed_value: int, amp: float = 3.5) -> Dictionary` returning
   `{"bed": PackedVector2Array, "verge": PackedVector2Array, "ruts": Array[PackedVector2Array], "tufts": Array[Dictionary]}`:
   - `verge`: the rect grown by 2 px with both long edges wobbling +-`amp` (reuse `trail_edge_polygon` if its shape fits);
   - `bed`: the rect inset ~2 px, edges wobbling by about half `amp`;
   - `ruts`: two polylines along the road at ~30% and ~70% of its width, each point jittered +-1 px, broken into
     segments (gaps every 40-120 px) so they read as worn tracks, not rails; none on rects narrower than 24 px;
   - `tufts`: ~1 per 18 px of length on each verge ({pos, h}), plus a sparse middle strip of grass (a few tufts) on
     side lanes only (width < 24 px).
   Straight (no wobble) within 10 px of a bridge rect, a map edge or a place's footprint, like GFX-178's streams.
2. `src/world/world.gd` `_draw_road()` (~2744): when the style is `grass` or `litter` (not paved/scree/bog), draw the
   verge (path colour darkened ~0.15, or the season's grass blend), the bed (path colour), then the ruts (bed darkened
   ~0.2, 1 px), then tufts (map grass colour) on top. Winter (`snowy`): the bed lerps toward snow like scree does, ruts
   stay darker (slush). Keep the existing `path_tiles` puddles/ruts for rain if they still read; drop any that clash.
3. Where a side lane meets the main road, end it in a short widening fan (2-3 px wider over its last 10 px) instead of a
   square end. Find how lanes are stored (map `paths` and GFX-176's stall lanes) and touch only `grass`/`litter` maps.
4. No data changes; no change for paved (Fallen Star Market), scree (ridge, Azure Peak) or bog (marsh) roads.

## Tests
`tests/unit/test_scenery*.gd` (the file that tests `stone_road`): the helper is deterministic for one seed; every
`bed` point lies inside `verge`'s bounding box; edges are not straight (some point deviates >= 1 px from the rect
edge); ruts absent below 24 px width; points within 10 px of a given bridge rect sit on the rect edge.

## Acceptance
Render `screenshot_first_hour.gd -- --season=Spring` (hud_arrival.png) and `--season=Winter`, plus
`--region=misty_forest`, at 1280x800 and open them: the road edges wander, ruts and verge tufts show, lanes end softly,
bridges still meet the road cleanly. Ground build time must not grow by more than ~10 ms per region (PERF-008's script
if it has landed; otherwise time `_ready` of the world before/after and put both numbers in the commit). tools/test.sh.
