# GFX-157: Fight and encounter backdrops that read as places

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's side-view battle backdrops (layered depth, varied silhouettes), look only.

## What is wrong (planner shots 2026-11-15 12:10, 1280x720, `screenshot_first_hour.gd` combat_report.png and encounter.png)
- **Qingshi fight (roofs):** the far layer draws as pale, near-white rounded blobs that read as clouds or snow drifts, not hills.
  Cause: `DuelStage._draw_backdrop` (src/ui/duel_stage.gd ~402) lerps `far` toward the haze twice
  (`.lerp(haze, 0.55).lerp(haze, 0.4)`, ~73% haze), and `hill_arc` gives every far hill the same round dome.
- **Woods encounter (pines):** the near layer is one row of identical grey triangles at an even pitch (a fence of triangles),
  all the same height, on a pale sky, with tiny pines only at the right end.
- The "Defeated" tag over a fallen fighter (~345) sits over the backdrop with no plate, so it reads as floating text.

## Do
1. **Far layer:** one haze lerp of about 0.45 (not two), and a far colour that keeps the region's hue (it must differ from
   the sky's lower colour by at least 0.12 in value, test it). Far hills get 2-3 overlapping arcs of different radius and
   height per piece (a ridge line, not domes); peaks regions keep their crag polygons.
2. **Near layer, pines:** each pine piece is a seeded tree of 2-3 stacked triangles (tiers) with a 2 px trunk; height varies
   60-140% of the base, pitch varies (no two neighbours the same gap), and 20-30% of the pieces are a darker second row
   drawn in front at the foot of the layer. No pine piece may be a single triangle.
3. **Near layer, roofs:** keep the houses, but give each a darker roof band and one lit wall tone so they read as buildings
   in front of the far ridge (today both are flat grey).
4. **Reeds and hills** (marsh, default): check them in the same pass (render `--fight-region` if QA-209 has landed,
   otherwise stage a fight with `GameState.current_region` set in a local scratch copy of the screenshot script, do not
   commit that copy) and fix only what reads wrong the same way (pale blobs, identical repeats).
5. **"Defeated" tag:** draw it on a small dark plate (UIStyle panel colour at 0.8 alpha, 4 px padding) above the fallen
   fighter so it reads over any backdrop.

All shapes stay deterministic (`unit_hash(rid, n)`), no global rng; `reduce_motion` is unaffected (static art).

## Tests (tests/unit/test_duel_stage.gd or the nearest existing duel stage test)
- `backdrop_layers` for a pines region: near pieces have at least 3 distinct heights and at least 2 distinct gaps.
- The far colour for qingshi_village and misty_forest differs from the sky's lower colour by >= 0.12 in value.
- Every pines piece carries >= 2 tiers (whatever field you add to the piece dictionary).
