Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's pixel beasts (look only: each animal reads from its silhouette alone).

## Problem (planner shot 2026-11-18 06:10, screens/fh_lost_fight.png)
The Mist Wolf in the fight scene looks like a grey boar. `PixelFigure.beast_frame` (src/world/pixel_figure.gd ~377)
draws wolf, boar, tiger, fox, deer and ram from the same `BEAST_ROWS` body (a long low barrel, short legs, the head at
the same height) and only adds a few pixels of marks in `_beast_marks` (ears, tusks, stripes). Ape, toad, crane, bird and
snake already have their own rows. The same frames draw the roaming beasts on the region maps
(src/world/interactables/roamer.gd ~262) and the foe in the fight stage (src/ui/duel_stage.gd ~388), so the fix shows
everywhere.
