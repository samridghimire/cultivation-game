# GFX-028: A duel stage you can see

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's side-on duel framing (big readable fighters on a strip of ground); mechanics/art
are ours.

## Why
Planner shot combat_report.png (2026-10-30 18:15): the fighters are ~30 px tall in a 600 px window, the Wild Boar is a dark
brown smear on the dark panel (`DuelStage.fur_for("boar")` = 3b3028 on a 14191f panel), and the band around them is empty.
The fight is the most exciting moment of the first hour and it reads as a text log.

## What (src/ui/duel_stage.gd, src/world/pixel_figure.gd, src/ui/combat_report.gd)
1. **Bigger fighters on ground.** Draw both fighters at an integer 3x of their texture (nearest filter), feet on a ground strip:
   a 24 px band of the current region's grass texture (reuse GFX-026's `GroundTiles` per-region texture if it can be fetched
   by region id; else a flat green/brown strip using the region's ground colour) with a soft oval shadow under each fighter.
   Grow the stage's `custom_minimum_size.y` so it fits (about 130 px); keep the report on screen at 1280x720 (the log scrolls).
2. **Beasts you can tell apart.** `PixelFigure.beast_frame` has one body. Add kinds by enemy id keyword: `wolf|fox|dog` (lean,
   pointed ears, long tail), `boar|pig` (heavy body, tusks), `tiger|leopard|cat` (stripes), `snake|serpent|python` (low coil),
   `bird|crow|hawk|crane` (wings), fallback = today's body. `beast_frame(fur, index, kind := "")`, cached by kind.
   `DuelStage.beast_kind(enemy_id) -> String` is static and tested. List every `beast`-tagged id in data/enemies.json in the
   commit and which kind it got.
3. **Contrast.** Every beast and robed foe gets a 1 px light rim (fur lightened 0.45) on its outline side facing the light, or
   a lighter outline colour, so a dark boar is visible on the dark panel. Boar fur may move to a mid brown (5a4636).
4. **Names over the bars.** "You" and the foe's name in small text above each hp bar (the left/right bars are unlabeled today).
5. Keep everything that exists: lunge, flash, shake, fallen pose (a beast on its back flips), reduce_motion.

## Tests (extend tests/unit/test_duel_stage.gd or the file that covers DuelStage)
- `beast_kind("wild_boar") == "boar"`, `"mist_wolf" -> "wolf"`, an unknown beast -> "".
- `PixelFigure.beast_frame(c, 0, kind)` returns distinct images for two kinds (compare a few pixels) and caches per kind.
- The stage's minimum height grew and fits in the combat report at 1280x720 (the report's Continue button stays on screen).
- The foe name label shows the enemy's display name.
