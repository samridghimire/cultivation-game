# GFX-258: The breakthrough, staged

Role: world-ui. Reference: Tale of Immortal's breakthrough scenes (the cultivator seated at the centre while qi gathers
and the heart demon rises); mechanics and staging only, never its art, names or layout.

## Why
A major breakthrough (Mortal -> Qi Refining, Qi Refining -> Foundation Establishment, ...) is the biggest moment of a
cultivation game, and today it is a plain text window (`src/ui/breakthrough_screen.gd`): title, blurb, vision choices,
chance, terms, risk, a pill row and Attempt / Not yet. Pressing Attempt closes the window at once; success opens the
text-only RealmCard and failure only flashes a banner (`hud.gd` `_on_breakthrough`). Planner shot 2026-11-26 06:10
(`screenshot_screens.gd -- --breakthrough-shots`, bs_b_pill.png / breakthrough_vision.png). The sect cards already have
picture headers (SectCard.SectVignette, ContestArena), so this window looks poorer than a sect chore.

## Scope (this task)
1. **`BreakthroughStage`** (new `src/ui/breakthrough_stage.gd`, `class_name BreakthroughStage extends Control`, `##` doc
   comment, static typing). A 480x128 picture drawn in `_draw()` at 2x like `SectCard.SectVignette`:
   - The player seated cross-legged on a round mat at the centre (reuse `PixelFigure.frame(look, "down", 0)`; the seated
     pose can be the standing frame with the bottom 6 rows cut and a darker lap band drawn over, as the time-skip
     overlay's meditation pose does; read `time_skip_overlay.gd` / `qi_gathering.gd` first and reuse their helper if one
     exists). The player's look comes from the same source SectVignette uses for `player_look`.
   - A backdrop that depends on the target realm index: a dark night sky with a few stars for every realm; from
     Foundation Establishment up add a faint mountain ridge line. Keep colours in `UIStyle` or local consts.
   - Qi motes: 12-20 small squares on slow spirals around the player (`_process` advances a phase; positions from a
     fixed formula, no rng, so screenshots are stable at a given phase). Colour from the player's strongest spiritual
     root element if there is a helper for element colours (grep `element_color`); otherwise UIStyle.ACCENT.
   - `vision` mode: when a heart vision is pending (`_vision_box.visible`), a dim, semi-transparent figure (same
     PixelFigure in a grey/violet tint, alpha ~0.45) stands to the right, facing the player. After the player answers
     the vision (`_face`), it fades out over ~0.6 s.
   - API: `setup(look: Dictionary, target_realm: int, vision: bool) -> void`, `fade_vision() -> void`,
     `play_attempt(success: bool) -> void` (see 3), signal `attempt_finished`.
2. **In the window.** `BreakthroughScreen` adds the stage at the top, under the title (above the blurb). `_refresh()`
   calls `setup(...)`; `_face()` calls `fade_vision()`. The window must still fit at 1280x720: if it overflows, shorten
   the stage to 96 px rather than dropping rows; check both resolutions.
3. **The attempt, animated.** Pressing Attempt (after the existing "Push on without facing it?" confirm) no longer
   closes the window at once: input is locked (Attempt/Not yet disabled), `GameState.attempt_breakthrough()` runs
   immediately (rules unchanged; the outcome is known), and the stage plays ~1.4 s:
   - all motes spiral into the player, then
   - success: a white-gold flash that fills the stage and fades, the player drawn brighter for the rest;
   - failure: the motes burst outward and fade, a short red flash, the player drawn darker.
   Then the window closes and the existing flow continues (RealmCard on success, the banner on failure, tribulation
   and death paths untouched). Listen to `EventBus.breakthrough_attempted(success, realm_name)` to know the outcome;
   if it never fires within the frame (e.g. `_can_act()` refused, a tribulation screen took over), close at once as
   today. Any confirm/cancel press during the animation skips to its end (never strands the player).
   The tribulation path (`tribulation_screen.gd`) is out of scope: if the attempt opens it, close immediately.
4. **Gamepad/keyboard:** focus rules unchanged (never starts on Attempt); during the animation focus stays on a
   disabled button, and the skip works with `ui_accept` / `ui_cancel`.
5. **Shot.** In `tests/sim/screenshot_screens.gd`'s `--breakthrough-shots`, also write `bs_stage_success.png` and
   `bs_stage_fail.png`, captured mid-animation (call a `debug_set_phase(t: float, success: bool)` on the stage instead
   of waiting real time). The existing bs_*.png now show the stage. Render at 1280x800 and 1280x720 and open every PNG
   before landing.

## Tests (tests/unit/test_breakthrough_stage.gd)
- `setup` with `vision = true` makes the ghost visible; `fade_vision` hides it after the fade (advance with
  `_process(1.0)`).
- `play_attempt(true)` emits `attempt_finished` after its duration (drive `_process` by hand); a skip call ends it at once.
- The mote positions are the same for the same phase (determinism) and stay inside the 480x128 rect.
- BreakthroughScreen: after Attempt with a forced outcome the window stays visible until `attempt_finished`, then
  closes; if `breakthrough_attempted` never fires it closes at once (test with a player who cannot attempt).
- Existing breakthrough-screen and focus-audit tests stay green.

## Acceptance
- The window shows a seated player with gathering qi; a pending vision shows the ghost, which fades once faced.
- Attempt plays a short success or failure beat before the realm card / failure banner.
- No rule changes (no balance run needed), fits 1280x720, gamepad works, `tools/test.sh` prints ALL CHECKS PASSED.
- Under ~350 changed lines. The realm card and a real failure card reuse this stage in GFX-260 (not here).
