# GFX-071: The gate spans the road, the court leaves room, the crane can be seen (world-ui)

Reference: Wandering Sword's town gates and sect courts (readable top-down pixel landmarks), mechanics only.
Source: planner shots 2026-11-05 12:15 (`screenshot_first_hour.gd -- --great --region=azure_peak,withered_bone_marsh,misty_forest`
at 1280x800), after GFX-069 and GFX-070 landed.

## What the shots show
1. **The gate floats above the road.** The main path runs east-west, the two posts stand north and south of it
   (right), but the tiled lintel is drawn as a horizontal bar above the north post only, so the roof hangs in the
   air beside the road and the south post stands alone. A pine (Azure) and a dead tree (marsh) are drawn on top of
   the name board.
2. **The Azure court covers the Cloud-Piercing Grotto.** GFX-070 grew the court south; the grotto (abode) and its
   label now sit on the court's bottom flagstones. `SectCourt._clear` only tests the places' `size` rects, which
   are smaller than the drawn landmark and ignore the label.
3. **The Great Cloud Crane is hidden.** Its own label and Hermit Gu's label are stacked right on top of its figure,
   so the one thing the player came to see on a great-beast day is a label pile.

## Do
### (1) A gate seen side-on (src/world/sect_court.gd `draw_gate`, `gate_rect`)
For a path wider than tall (every gate today), draw the gate as seen from the south with the road passing through it:
- keep the two posts on the path's north and south edges (as now);
- the roof: a tiled band **running north-south from the north post's top to the south post's top** (x = gx +- 14,
  from `top - 38` to `bottom - 38`), with the upturned ends sticking out 8 px past each post, the light ridge line on
  its west side, tile strokes across it; draw the south post after the roof so it reads in front;
- the name board moves to a **stone stele beside the north post** (west of it when the gate is on the left side of
  the map, east when on the right), facing the viewer: the same board look and text, on a short plinth;
- if a path is taller than wide (none today), keep today's horizontal drawing.
Update `gate_rect` to the new footprint (roof band, posts, stele). In world.gd `_build_decor` drop any decor whose
rect (pos +- 16 px) intersects `SectCourt.gate_rect(g).grow(12)`.

### (2) The court leaves room (sect_court.gd `court_rect` / `_clear`)
`_clear` must test each other place's **drawn** footprint and its label: grow the place's `size` rect to the landmark
draw size (use the same size PixelLandmark/PixelPlace draws, or `size` x 1.3 when that is not reachable from pure code)
plus a 22 px band above it for the label, and keep 12 px clearance. If no court scale fits, try shifting the court
north by up to 40 px before falling back to the plain hall rect. The hall must stay in the court's north half
(GFX-070's test).

### (3) Labels do not cover a great beast (src/world/world.gd label spread, src/world/label_layout.gd)
The great beast's figure rect (its sprite bounds, about 48x40 at the crane) becomes a **blocker** for every label
except its own, like the [E] chip (GFX-069); its own label is placed above the figure, never on it. Same for the
current target's figure.

## Tests (tests/unit/)
- Gate: for azure_peak and withered_bone_marsh, `gate_rect` contains both posts and the stele; the roof band's x
  range is within 24 px of the gate x (it spans the road, not across the map); no decor rect intersects the gate rect.
- Court: for every region with a sect hall, `court_rect` does not intersect any other place's grown footprint+label rect;
  hall in the north half (keep GFX-070's test).
- Labels: with a great beast figure rect and two labels placed on it, `LabelLayout.resolve` moves or fades the other
  label off it.
