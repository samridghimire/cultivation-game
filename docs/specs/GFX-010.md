# GFX-010: A HUD that leaves the world visible

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword (a small status corner plus a quest tracker on the right; the town fills the screen)
and Tale of Immortal (bars and numbers instead of sentences). Mechanics and layout ideas only, never their art or exact layout.

## Why
Planner screenshots of every region at 1280x800 (2026-10-29, `tests/sim/screenshot_regions.gd`): the status panel is 340 px
wide and ~530 px tall, and the message log is a 520x180 box, mostly empty. Together they cover about 27% of the screen, the
whole left third. In Qingshi they hide the pond, a house, the Old Quarry Tailings and NPCs; in Azure Peak the Mountain Path.
The world is what the owner wants to look good (DESIGN "Art direction"), so the HUD should get out of its way.

## What to build (src/ui/hud.gd, src/ui/ui_style.gd, src/autoload/settings.gd, src/ui/settings_screen.gd)
Keep every Label that exists today and every string it shows (tests and `--hint-shot` asserts read them). Only regroup.

1. **Status card** (top-left, position (16,16), width 300, built in `_build_status_panel`):
   - row 1: `_age` (name, age, lifespan cue) as today;
   - row 2: new `_realm` Label (15 px, `UIStyle.ACCENT`) = `Cultivation.realm_label(p, data)` (moved out of `_status`);
   - row 3: `_qi_bar` 300x16 with a new child Label `_qi_text` centred on it (12 px) = `"Qi: %d / %d"` (moved out of `_status`);
   - row 4: an HBoxContainer with `_lives` and a new `_currency` Label (`currency_line(p)`, moved out of `_status`),
     separated by 16 px; the lives badge stays on `_lives`;
   - row 5: `_status` (13 px, grey `Color(0.75, 0.76, 0.8)`) keeps alignment, sect, date/season and the region line with
     its qi multiplier, event suffix and "(Secret realm open)" exactly as built today;
   - then `_bottleneck`, `_next_layer`, `_injuries` as today.
2. **Guide card** (new PanelContainer `_guide_panel`, top-right: anchors right, offset_right -16, top 16, width 340):
   a dim 12 px header "Next steps", then `_goal`, `_hunt`, `_fair`, `_hint` (moved from the status card, same widths minus
   nothing: 316 px). Hidden when all four are hidden (or `hud_hints` is 0). The hint badge and `_shrink_hint` keep working;
   `_fit_status_panel` becomes `_fit_panels`: the status card drops `_next_layer` first when too tall, the guide card drops
   `_fair`, then shrinks `_hint`, then `_goal`, using the same height limit (viewport height - LOG_HEIGHT - margins).
3. **Key bar**: out of the status card into its own strip (`_key_bar`, 12 px, `Color(0.7, 0.7, 0.7, 0.85)`) anchored
   bottom-right (offset_right -16, offset_bottom -8), max width 600, wrapping to at most 2 lines. New setting
   `key_help` (bool, default true) in `Settings.DEFAULTS` + `validate` + the settings screen ("Key help on the HUD");
   F1 help is always available, so the strip may be turned off.
4. **Interact prompt** (`_prompt`): keep it bottom-centre, but make sure it sits above the key bar strip (no overlap).
5. **Message log**: `LOG_HEIGHT` 196 -> 124 (about 5 lines at 14 px; keep the 520 width). Idle fade: remember the time of
   the last `_on_message`; after 8 s without a message the log's target alpha is `min(current target, 0.55)`; a new message,
   mouse hover or the log screen (L) restores 1.0. Combine with `_compute_fade_targets` (the "player behind the panel" fade
   to 0.35 still wins). Add the guide card as a third fade target (it can also cover the player): `_fade_targets` becomes a
   Vector3 or three floats.
6. `_update_modal` hides the guide card and key bar behind modals like the status card.

## Tests (tests/unit/test_hud_layout.gd, new; see tests/unit/test_time_skip.gd or test_banner_queue.gd for building a HUD)
- Start a session, build the HUD, refresh; at the 1280x800 viewport the rects of status card, guide card, log, key bar and
  prompt (prompt made visible with a target name) do not intersect pairwise.
- The status card and guide card each fit above the log (bottom <= viewport height - LOG_HEIGHT - STATUS_MARGIN).
- Status card + guide card + log area < 18% of the viewport area (today ~27%).
- `_hint.text` still contains the first `Guidance.hints` line; `_status.text` no longer contains "Qi: " and `_qi_text.text` does.
- `key_help = false` hides `_key_bar`; Settings rejects a non-bool.
- The idle fade: after `_on_message` the log target alpha is 1.0; with the last-message time 9 s in the past it is <= 0.55
  (expose the time as a variable or pass `now` into a static helper, e.g. `static func log_idle_alpha(seconds_since: float) -> float`).
