# GFX-236: The oath, with people in it

Role: world-ui. Reference: Stardew Valley's top-heart events and xianxia sworn-sibling oaths (look only, never its art or layout).

## What is wrong (planner shot 2026-11-22 06:10, `--sworn=hunter_ma`, 1280x800)
- The oath is staged in the upper middle of the screen (around 640,260), away from the player, who stands below it.
- The player and Hunter Ma are two flat rectangles (blue and tan, about 64x88 px) with a skin-coloured strip on top: no faces, no
  clothes, nothing like the world sprites a few pixels away.
- Place labels and name plates ("Elder", "Village Pond Jetty (0/3)", "Meditation Rock") are drawn through the figures.
- The card itself reads well: "Hunter Ma: Sworn friend" and the tier line. Keep it.

## Goal
The oath happens where the player stands, with the real player and villager figures, beside an incense burner drawn in the world.
The overlay only dims, draws the smoke and shows the card.

## How (follow the tea/lend staging, which already works)
`src/ui/time_skip_overlay.gd` `_begin_lend(npc_id, tea_id)` / `_end_lend()` already: find the world NPC node, pick a free spot beside
the player with `Scenery.lend_spot(region, pos, gap)`, call `npc.set_tea(true, spot)` / `set_lending(true)`, sit the player with
`player.set_seated(true)`, and restore everything at the end. Reuse that pattern (extract a small shared helper if it keeps both
readable; don't duplicate 40 lines).

1. `src/ui/sworn_oath.gd`
   - `begin(npc_name, tier)` becomes `begin(npc_id: String, npc_name: String, tier: Dictionary)`; update the call in
     `hud.gd _on_friendship_reached` (line ~1871).
   - Remove `_draw_figure` and the block figures. Keep the dim, the card, the timing (`DURATION`, `BOW_START`, `BOW_LENGTH`),
     the skip on any confirm/cancel press and the `finished` signal.
   - Draw the three smoke threads at the burner's **screen** position (world point -> screen via the viewport canvas transform,
     `get_viewport().get_canvas_transform() * world_pos`), so the smoke rises from the world burner.
   - New `const GAP := 72.0` (px between the two figures' feet; the burner sits in the middle). `layout(centre, gap)` stays and
     is called with the player's world position as one end: player at `player_pos`, friend at the spot from `Scenery.lend_spot`,
     burner at their midpoint.
2. Staging (in hud.gd or a helper next to `_begin_lend`):
   - On begin: find the NPC node (same lookup `_begin_lend` uses), move it to the lend spot facing the player, both figures face
     each other. If the NPC node is not in the current region (the tier was reached elsewhere), skip staging and play only the
     dim + card (no figures, no burner); that path must not crash.
   - On finish or skip: restore the NPC (`set_lending(false)` / back to wandering as `_end_lend` does) and the player.
3. Bow: add `set_bow(on: bool)` to `src/world/player.gd` and `src/world/interactables/npc.gd`: while on, the body is drawn 2 art px
   lower (the same offset `set_seated` uses for its body, without folding the legs). SwornOath turns it on at `BOW_START` and off
   at `BOW_START + BOW_LENGTH` (`bow_amount` already gives the timing).
4. Burner prop: `WorkProps.draw_burner(canvas, centre, scale)` in `src/world/work_props.gd` (a squat bronze tripod ~12x10 art px,
   dark rim, a glowing ember dot), drawn by the NPC node (like the tea table under `_tea`) at the midpoint while an
   `_oath` flag is on (`npc.set_oath(on, toward)` mirroring `set_tea`).
5. Labels: while the oath plays, hide place labels, name plates and the [E] prompt. Interactables already have per-label alpha
   (`interactable.gd` `refresh_label_alpha`, `label_drawn`); add one static flag (e.g. `Interactable.labels_suppressed := true`)
   that makes `label_drawn()` false and the prompt hidden, set on begin and cleared on finish/skip. Make sure a skip mid-bow
   also clears it (test).
6. `reduce_motion` setting: no bow movement and a single smoke frame (as GFX-226 does for steam).

## Tests (`tests/unit/test_sworn_oath.gd`, extend the existing one)
- `layout(player_pos, GAP)` puts the player end at `player_pos` and the burner at the midpoint.
- After `begin` then a skip, `Interactable.labels_suppressed` is false and the NPC is no longer in oath/lending state
  (build a minimal NPC node as other npc tests do).
- `begin` with an npc id that has no world node finishes without error and still emits `finished`.
- `bow_amount` unchanged.
