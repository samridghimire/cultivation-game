# GFX-142: Ponds with real shores, places without plates

Role: world-ui. Reference: Stardew Valley's ponds and Wandering Sword's map props (look only, no art taken).

## Problem (planner shots 2026-11-14, 1280x720)
- GFX-135 gave ponds a ragged mask, but `Scenery.pond_edge` only varies the radius between 0.88 and 1.0 of the ellipse, so
  every jetty pond (Village Pond Jetty, Misty Pond Jetty, Bog Pond Shore) still reads as a clean ellipse.
- Frozen ponds draw their 2 px bank as a modulo pattern (`(x * 5 + y * 3) % 7 < 5` in `PixelLandmark.pond_image`), which
  shows as a dotted white ring round the ice.
- GFX-137 Follow-ups: `PixelPlace` `_cave`, `_meditation`, `_gather_crater`, `_crystal`, `_frost`, `_terrace` and the
  `PlaceArt` vector fallbacks still stand on plain `ell()` ellipses; only Waterfall Cave's abode base changed.
- The Blood Lotus Reed Hall's plank platform (GFX-136) is still a big near-rectangle.

## Changes
1. `Scenery.pond_edge(angle, seed)`: 0.72..1.0, built from 2-3 low-frequency lobes (period >= 90 degrees) plus the existing
   small ripple, so each pond has one or two coves and a bulge. Keep it deterministic per seed. Make sure
   `Scenery.jetty_layout` (the jetty must still start on the bank and reach open water) and `pond_water_contains` and any
   fish/ripple placement (src/world/water_life.gd) use the same edge; adjust their tests if the numbers move.
2. Bank: 3-5 px wide (seeded per angle) instead of a fixed 2 px; frozen bank = snow drifts from a seeded value-noise
   threshold (patches 3-8 px), with the earth showing between; no modulo patterns anywhere in pond_image.
3. Replace the plain `ell()` bases in the six PixelPlace functions above with `PixelPlace.blob` (GFX-137) in each one's
   ground tone (worn earth, rock, frost, terrace soil), and in PlaceArt's fallbacks use `Scenery.ragged_outline` polygons.
4. Reed Hall platform: build its outline from `Scenery.ragged_outline` with a larger amplitude and one or two notches
   (a missing corner, a short jetty of boards toward the nearest pool), so it is no longer a rectangle.

## Tests (extend tests/unit/test_water_shapes.gd)
- For 20 seeds, the pond edge spans at least 0.18 between its min and max over 360 samples; still within 0.72..1.0.
- The jetty for each of the three jetty places starts on bank and its end is on open water (`pond_water_contains`).
- `pond_image(frozen=true)`: no 7-pixel periodic pattern on the bank (count snow pixels per 7-px window along the rim;
  the variance is non-zero) — or a simpler check that two seeds give different bank snow pixels.
- `grep`-style test or a unit check that the six PixelPlace functions no longer call `ell(` for their base (or assert
  their returned commands contain a blob command).

and open them: ponds with coves, no dotted ring on ice, the Reed Hall platform ragged. tools/test.sh. Under ~400 lines.
