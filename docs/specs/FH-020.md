# FH-020 Give Qingshi Village a working economy (content)

## Problem
In Qingshi the workshop offers 5 craft recipes, but no ingredient (herbs, ores) is sold or gatherable in the village. Talisman
paper is sold, iron essence is not. The only income after the chores is "Work as Doctor" (~5 stones a month). Guidance tells the
player to "work at a workshop", which is a dead end here. The Azure Cloud rank-0 mission board offers wolf hunts (lethal and
Deadly for a newcomer) and herb hand-ins the player can't fill in Qingshi.

## Change (data only, plus any validation the new fields need; no new fields expected)
1. data/regions.json, region qingshi: add a `gather` place "Village Herb Slope" (pick a free position; run
   test_region_layout / place-overlap tests, see HANDOFF lesson about two places at the same position) with a low-grade
   gather_table: the basic alchemy herbs used by the village recipes (find them in data/recipes.json for the recipes the
   workshop in Qingshi offers; e.g. spirit_herb, dew_grass), weight for "" (nothing) about 30%, 1-2 per find. Also add the
   lowest-grade ore used by the first forge recipe (e.g. iron_essence) at low weight, or to the village merchant stock.
2. The village merchant: stock the cheapest ingredients for the first 2 alchemy recipes at a price such that crafting and
   selling the result is a small profit (check Items.sell_price and the G-005e buyback rule: crafted goods sell for at most
   material cost x a factor in recipes.json, so the profit comes from the Alchemist's own use and rank xp, not resale).
3. data/sect_missions.json: one rank-0 mission per sect that a Qi Refining 1st Layer newcomer can do in Qingshi or the sect's
   region without lethal combat: e.g. Azure Cloud "Gather herbs for the Medicine Hall" (hand in 3 of the herb from step 1),
   Myriad Treasure "Carry goods to the market" (days only), Blood Lotus "Collect a debt" (non-lethal Mountain Bandit). Use
   existing mission fields only (read the file's `_doc`), modest contribution (20-40), 15-30 day cooldowns.
4. help.json is FH-022; don't touch it here.

## Tests
- Existing data validation and test_obtainability / test_data_references must pass.
- Add a test (tests/unit/test_first_hour_economy.gd): in Qingshi, every ingredient of at least one alchemy recipe offered by the
  Qingshi workshop is obtainable in Qingshi (sold by a merchant there or in a Qingshi gather table without min_realm).
- Each sect has at least one rank-0 mission with no enemy or a non-lethal enemy.
