# GFX-105: A title screen that looks like the game

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's pixel title scenes (mechanics/presentation idea only: a still pixel landscape
with a little motion; never its names, layout or art).

## Problem (planner shot 2026-11-09, `screenshot_screens.gd -- --title --creation`)
`title.png`: smooth vector hills, a big flat moon with soft rings, grey translucent bars for mist, the title in a plain
font, seven flat buttons stacked in the middle. It is the first thing a player sees and it is the least "pixel art" screen
in the game, while the world itself is now pixel art (PixelPlace, PixelFigure, FramedPanel).

## Build
Files: `src/ui/title_backdrop.gd` (redraw), `src/ui/main_menu.gd` (layout), `tests/unit/test_title_backdrop.gd`.
Everything is code-drawn (no binary assets), like PixelPlace: draw into an `Image` at low resolution, then show it as a
`TextureRect`/`draw_texture_rect` with `TEXTURE_FILTER_NEAREST`, integer scale (`PX := 4`, so a 320x200 canvas fills
1280x800; for other window sizes scale by `floor(min(w/320, h/200))` and fill the rest with the sky colour).

1. **The scene (static, cached once per size):** night sky with a 3-band dithered gradient (deep blue -> violet near the
   horizon); a pixel moon (a 20 px disc, two lighter craters, 1 px rim), no soft rings; 30-40 single-pixel stars from a
   fixed-seed `RandomNumberGenerator` (seed 7, never global randf); three ranges of mountains with jagged pixel ridges,
   each range darker and nearer (ridge heights from a seeded 1D noise or summed sines, quantised to whole pixels); on the
   middle range a small pavilion on a peak (stacked roof of 2 tiers with upturned eaves, 2 red pillars, a lit window
   pixel), 6-10 pine silhouettes on the near range, and a tiny robed figure (reuse `PixelFigure.frame(look, "up", 0)`
   at 1x, or a 5x8 silhouette) standing on a rock in the foreground looking at the pavilion.
2. **Motion (cheap, in `_process`):** 2-3 long pixel cloud strips drifting left at 2-6 px/s (wrap around), and 3-4 stars
   that twinkle (toggle brightness on a timer). Respect the existing reduced-motion setting if `Settings` has one
   (grep `reduce_motion`); with it on, nothing moves.
3. **Title plaque (main_menu.gd):** the title text on a `UIStyle.panel_style()` FramedPanel plaque (gold frame), font size
   as today; the tagline under it in the dim gold accent. Keep the title string wherever it comes from today.
4. **Buttons:** a narrower column (~320 px wide instead of ~540), placed in the lower-left third over the darkest
   foreground (so the pavilion and moon stay visible); disabled Continue/Load stay visibly disabled. Focus order and
   `grab_focus()` unchanged (gamepad). Esc/back behaviour unchanged.
5. Character creation (`character_creation.gd`) uses the same backdrop automatically; do not restyle its panel here
   (that is WU-497).

## Tests
- `test_title_backdrop.gd`: the cached image has the expected low-res size for 1280x800 and 1920x1080 (integer scale 4 and
  5 -> 320x200 canvas with letterbox fill), drawing twice with the same seed gives identical pixels (determinism), and with
  reduced motion the cloud offset does not change after `_process(1.0)`.
