# GFX-135: Water that looks like water

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's ponds and rivers, Stardew Valley's shorelines (mechanics and readability only, no art).

## Problem (planner shots 2026-11-13 18:10, 1280x720)
Every pond is a perfect ellipse with a hard dark outline (Qingshi's Village Pond Jetty, Misty Pond Jetty, Bog Pond Shore,
Corpse-Lily Bog). Frozen ponds are pale perfect ellipses. Misty Forest's stream is a ruler-straight vertical band.
After GFX-133 the ground is ragged and natural, so the water now looks like a sticker on top of it.

## What to build
1. **Ragged shorelines.** `PixelLandmark.texture("pond", ...)` (src/world/pixel_landmark.gd ~28) and `frozen_pond()` build the
   pond from a seeded ragged mask instead of an ellipse test. Add a pure helper in `src/core/systems/scenery.gd`:
   `static func pond_mask_radius(angle: float, seed: int, amp: float) -> float` (returns a radius factor 1.0 +/- amp, smoothed
   seeded noise over ~10-14 control points round the circle, same seed = same shape) and use it per pixel:
   a pixel is water when its normalised elliptical distance < `pond_mask_radius(angle, seed, 0.12)`. Seed from the landmark's
   `pos` (hash(str(pos))) so it is stable per pond. Keep the ellipse's overall size so collision, jetty placement
   (`Scenery.jetty_layout`) and the GFX-125 lily pads still land on water: clamp the factor to [0.86, 1.0] at the jetty's angle
   (the jetty must still touch water) and test it.
2. **A bank, not an outline.** Replace the 1-2 px dark outline with a 2-3 px band in a muddy/sandy colour derived from the
   region's ground (`clear` water: sandy brown; `murky` marsh water: dark mud), a 1 px darker wet edge inside it, and a lighter
   shallows ring (water colour lightened ~0.15) 3-4 px wide inside the bank. Deep middle stays the water tint.
3. **Bank life.** Up to 6 small stones and 3-5 reed clumps on the bank band per pond (seeded, kept off the jetty, off paths and
   off label bands; reuse the GFX-125 reed drawing and its winter snow caps). Marsh (`water: murky`) gets more reeds, fewer stones.
4. **Frozen ponds.** Same ragged mask; ice is pale blue-grey with 2-4 seeded crack lines and a white snow drift on the
   bank band (not a uniform white rim). The GFX-114 fishing hole stays where it is.
5. **Meandering streams.** In `PixelLandmark` stream textures and `water_life.gd` `_draw` (bank lines are straight `draw_rect`s
   today, ~113-119), offset both banks by a smooth seeded sine (amplitude <= 6 px, wavelength 120-200 px) so the stream wobbles
   while its collision rect stays the same. Plank bridges (world.gd ~2186-2232) must still span the water: bring the wobble to 0
   within 24 px of any path crossing, and test that.

## Tests (tests/unit/test_water_shapes.gd, new)
- `pond_mask_radius` is deterministic per seed, stays within [1 - amp, 1 + amp], and differs between two seeds.
- For every pond landmark in data/regions.json: the jetty's water end is on water under the ragged mask; every lily pad
  position is on water.
- A stream's bank offset is 0 within 24 px of every path crossing in every region.
