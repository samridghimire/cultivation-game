# GFX-217: Days you can see pass (world-ui)

Reference: Stardew Valley's evening tint and lit windows; Tale of Immortal's seclusion time-lapse (look only).

## Why
Time only passes when an action takes it (GameClock is day-based). Meditating a month, travelling 3 days or gathering all
show the TimeSkipOverlay card (src/ui/time_skip_overlay.gd, FILL_SECONDS 0.6 + HOLD 1.8 s) over a world that never
changes light. The world has no dusk, night or dawn at all, so days feel like menu ticks.

## Do
1. **Pure curve** in a new `src/core/systems/day_light.gd` (`class_name DayLight`, static, no Nodes):
   `static func tint_at(phase: float, base: Color) -> Color` where phase 0..1 is one day (0 = morning as the game
   normally looks, 0.35 dusk = warm orange multiply ~(1.0, 0.82, 0.68), 0.5 night = blue multiply ~(0.45, 0.5, 0.75),
   0.85 dawn = pink-gold ~(0.95, 0.85, 0.85), 1.0 = base again), smoothly interpolated and multiplied onto `base`
   (the current season tint). `static func cycles_for(days: int) -> int` = clamp(days, 1, 3) (a month of seclusion shows
   at most 3 quick cycles), `static func is_night(phase: float) -> bool` for 0.4..0.75.
2. **World hook** in src/world/world.gd: on `EventBus.time_skipped(days, summary)` (already emitted), if `days >= 1` and the
   Ambient effects setting is on and not reduce_motion, tween `_season_tint.color` through `DayLight.tint_at` for
   `cycles_for(days)` cycles over the overlay's visible time (~0.6 s fill + up to 1.8 s hold; one cycle should take
   >= 0.7 s, so cap the total at ~2.2 s), ending exactly on `_tint_for(_season)`. A season change during the skip
   (the existing tween at ~line 208) must still end on the new season's tint: run the day cycle first, then the season
   tween, never two tweens on the same property at once (kill the old one).
   Reduce_motion or Ambient effects off: no cycle (today's behaviour).
3. **Lights at night.** While `DayLight.is_night(phase)`: existing lantern landmarks (`kind: "lantern"`, Fallen Star has 9)
   and house windows draw a small warm glow (a 10-16 px soft circle, additive-looking, drawn on a node that the
   CanvasModulate does not darken: e.g. a CanvasLayer-free Node2D with `light_mask`/a separate `CanvasItem` using
   `material = CanvasItemMaterial` with `light_mode = LIGHT_MODE_UNSHADED`, or simply draw with a colour that compensates;
   pick the simplest that renders right and say which in the commit). Only lights inside the viewport. Off outside the
   cycle.
4. The TimeSkipOverlay's dim (`Color(0,0,0,0.45)`) hides most of this: lower the dim to 0.25 while a cycle runs
   so the world shows through, keep text contrast (UIStyle panel behind the text is fine).

## Tests
- tests/unit/test_day_light.gd: `tint_at(0, c) == c` and `tint_at(1, c) == c`; night is darker than dusk is darker than
  morning (luminance); `cycles_for(0..40)` stays in 1..3; `is_night` true at 0.5, false at 0 and 0.2.
- A world test (like the GFX-215/GFX-214 ones): emitting `time_skipped(5, {})` with ambient effects on ends with
  `_season_tint.color == _tint_for(season)` after the tween finishes (advance with `await` frames or call the tween's
  custom_step); with reduce_motion the tint never changes.

## Acceptance
Add `--skip-dusk` to tests/sim/screenshot_first_hour.gd that pauses mid-cycle at night (phase 0.5) and renders
`skip_night.png` for Qingshi and Fallen Star (`--region=qingshi_village,fallen_star_market`). Open both at 1280x800:
the world is clearly night-blue, lanterns/windows glow, the HUD and overlay text are not tinted. `tools/test.sh`.
No balance impact (look only).
