# GFX-180: A woodland edge, not a hedge

Role: world-ui. Reference: Stardew Valley's map borders (look only: layered, irregular foliage with depth, never a row of
identical shapes). Planner shot 2026-11-17 `hud_arrival.png` (Qingshi summer, 1280x800).

## Problem
Qingshi Village is the only region with `map.edge = "hedge"` (data/regions.json). `Scenery.edge_props` (src/core/systems/scenery.gd ~640)
cuts each edge into 18-34 px steps, and `world.gd _draw_edge` (~1731) draws every `hedge` prop as the same three circles
(shadow, body, highlight) of radius 0.6 x min(rect) with an occasional pink dot. The result along the left and bottom edges
is a single row of near-identical green balls at an even pitch: the first thing a new player sees reads as a fence of beads.

## What to build (draw-only, seeded, no data changes needed)
In `world.gd _draw_edge`, case `"hedge"` (move the drawing into a helper `_draw_hedge_prop(it: Dictionary, winter: bool)` if it helps):
1. **Two layers of depth.** Per prop draw a back row and a front row: 2-4 overlapping blobs per prop (not one), radii varied
   0.45-0.85 x the prop's short side, centres jittered along the edge and 0-6 px inward, the back blobs darker and offset
   outward (toward the map edge) so the border reads as thick foliage that the map sits inside.
2. **Mixed plants.** About 1 in 5 props (by seed) becomes a small tree poking above the hedge (reuse the round-tree draw used
   by the decor pass, scaled ~0.8) and about 1 in 6 gets a flowering shrub (pink/white dots, spring and summer only) or a grey rock at its foot.
3. **Irregular inner silhouette.** The inward-facing outline must not be a straight line: let blob centres vary in inward
   offset (0..EDGE_INSIDE*0.5) so the edge undulates. Keep every prop inside its rect grown by 8 px (paths stay clear: the
   gaps from `edge_props` are unchanged).
4. **Colour.** Three greens per season from the region's `decor.tree_color` / a darkened ground colour instead of the fixed
   `3f7a35`; autumn tints some blobs ochre/rust (like GFX trees), winter keeps the snow caps on the top blobs.
5. Keep the cost sane: no more than ~6 draw calls per prop.

Do not change other edge kinds (treeline, wall, cliff, reeds). Do not change `edge_props` rect layout unless needed;
if you do, update tests/unit/test_edge_props.gd.

## Tests
- tests/unit/test_edge_props.gd (or a new test): the hedge variant choice is deterministic per seed, and across the
  Qingshi edge at least 3 different variants (plain, tree, flowering/rock) occur.
- If you add a pure helper (e.g. `Scenery.hedge_blobs(rect, seed, side) -> Array[Dictionary]`) put it in scenery.gd and
  test that every blob lies within rect.grow(8) and that blob radii are not all equal.

## Acceptance
Render `-- --season=Summer`, `--season=Autumn`, `--season=Winter` first-hour shots and `--region=qingshi_village --edge`
at 1280x800, open them: the left and bottom borders read as layered woodland with varied shapes and no visible
repeating pitch; paths still exit cleanly; labels near the edge (Village Hen Coop, Xiao Ling) stay readable.
tools/test.sh prints ALL CHECKS PASSED.
