# GFX-130: Ground with life

Role: world-ui. Reference: Stardew Valley's and Wandering Sword's ground (clumps, worn patches, tonal variety; mechanics and
composition only, never their art).

## Why (planner shots 2026-11-13 06:10 at 1280x720: hud_arrival.png, region_azure_peak.png, region_withered_bone_marsh.png)
Every region's ground is one flat colour with fine per-tile speckle (`GroundTiles.make_variant` / `ground_texture`). At 1280x720 a
whole screen is the same green (Qingshi) or grey-green (Azure Peak, marsh), so buildings look dropped on a lawn and the eye has
nothing to rest on between places. Real ground has large soft patches of lighter and darker grass, clover, bare earth where
people walk, and thicker growth at the edges.

## What to build (src/world/ground_tiles.gd; pure static helpers, deterministic, no global rng)
1. `GroundTiles.patch_shade(rid: String, x: int, y: int) -> float` in -1..1: low-frequency value noise (two octaves, cell sizes
   ~96 px and ~40 px, bilinear or smoothstep interpolation between hashed lattice values `hash("%s|%d|%d" % [rid, cx, cy])`).
   In `ground_texture` for styles grass, litter and bog, tint each pixel's base toward `base.lightened(0.10)` when the shade
   is > 0.35 and toward `base.darkened(0.12)` when < -0.35 (smooth ramp, never a hard edge across a 16 px tile). Speckle stays.
2. **Clover and moss patches**: 6-12 per region (seeded), 20-48 px soft blobs of a slightly bluer/yellower green with 1 px
   leaf dots (grass), or darker moss with a few lighter pixels (bog/litter). Skip any whose rect touches a path rect or a
   paved zone (pass the region's path rects into `ground_texture` through the existing `zones`-style argument or a new
   optional `avoid: Array` parameter defaulting to `[]`, so other callers do not change).
3. **Worn ground at busy places**: for each place in the region, a soft ellipse ~1.3x its keepout (`Npcs.place_keepout`) where
   grass thins: blend 35% toward the region's path colour with ragged edges (noise from step 1). Skip it for places whose look
   already draws its own ground (sect court, market plaza: anything inside `Scenery.paved_zones`).
4. **Seasons**: autumn turns the light patches golden-brown and the dark ones russet (reuse `_apply_season`'s tints); in winter the
   noise thins and thickens the snow (snow amount 0.75..1.0 by patch shade) so snow is not one flat sheet, and worn ground shows
   a little earth through the snow. Spring adds no new colour.
5. Build time: the texture is made once per region build. Measure `ground_texture` for a 1600x1000 region before and after
   (Time.get_ticks_usec in a scratch run) and keep the increase under ~60 ms; cache like `cached()` if needed. Paste the numbers.

## Tests (tests/unit/test_ground_life.gd)
- `patch_shade` is deterministic and stays in -1..1; over a 40x25 grid of samples 96 px apart both > 0.35 and < -0.35 occur.
- Neighbouring pixels differ by at most a small step (no hard seams): |shade(x) - shade(x+1)| < 0.08 across 200 random points.
- A clover patch never overlaps a given path rect (call the patch layout function directly; make it a pure static
  `clover_rects(rid, size, avoid) -> Array[Rect2]`).
- A region with style `paved` or `scree` returns the old texture unchanged (compare a few pixels with the pre-change code path, or
  assert the new helpers are not applied for those styles).
