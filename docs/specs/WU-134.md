# WU-134: The breakthrough window (the road to Foundation)

**Role:** world-ui. **After:** FOUND-002 (uses `Guidance.breakthrough_risk_text`; if FOUND-002 is not on main yet, wait).

## Why
Breaking through to Foundation Establishment is the biggest moment of the first years (35% base chance, a 300-stone pill,
30% of your qi lost on failure). Today it is one line in the meditation spot's menu: "Attempt breakthrough (35% chance)",
and the click rolls immediately. A held Foundation Establishment Pill must be used from the inventory first, and nothing
says so at the moment of choice. Tribulation breakthroughs (Core Formation+) already get a prepare screen
(`src/ui/tribulation_screen.gd`); non-tribulation major breakthroughs past Qi Refining should get a calmer version.

## What to build
New `src/ui/breakthrough_screen.gd` (`class_name BreakthroughScreen extends Control`, built in code, `UIStyle` look,
`##` doc comment, `signal closed`, `open()` / `close()`), registered in `src/ui/hud.gd` the way the tribulation screen is
(a member created in `_ready`, opened from a new `EventBus.breakthrough_prepare_requested` signal; add the signal to
`src/autoload/event_bus.gd` next to `tribulation_prepare_requested`).

Layout (one panel, centred, fits 1280x720):
1. Title: "Breakthrough: <next realm name>".
2. The chance as a big line: "<N>% chance".
3. The terms of `Cultivation.chance_breakdown(player, data)`, one per line: "<label>  +<N>%" (negative values "-N%").
4. The risk line: `Guidance.breakthrough_risk_text(player, data)` (FOUND-002), danger colour.
5. For each id in `Guidance.breakthrough_items(player, data)`: a button "Take <item name> (+<N>%)" that calls
   `GameState.use_item(id)` and then refreshes the window (the chance and terms change; the pill leaves the list).
   When the list is empty and `Guidance.pill_source_hint(player, data)` is not "", show that hint as a dim line instead.
6. Buttons: "Attempt" (calls `GameState.attempt_breakthrough()`, then closes) and "Not yet" (closes). Cancel / B also closes.

Focus: on open, `grab_focus()` the first "Take ..." button if any, else "Not yet" (never default to Attempt, so a
double-press cannot roll by accident). Every button reachable with the d-pad (set focus neighbours or use a VBox).

## Wiring
In `src/world/interactables/meditation_spot.gd` (~line 20), the non-tribulation branch: when the next realm index is
>= 2 (Foundation Establishment and up; Mortal -> Qi Refining stays a one-click 100%-ish step), the option's action
becomes `EventBus.breakthrough_prepare_requested.emit` (drop `keep_open`, like the tribulation branch). Keep the label and
description as they are.

## Tests (tests/unit/test_breakthrough_screen.gd)
- With a Qi Refining 9th Layer player at the bottleneck holding a foundation_establishment_pill: open() shows a "Take" button;
  pressing it removes the pill from the inventory, sets `player.breakthrough_pill`, and the window's chance line rises by 25.
- With no pill held: no Take button, and the pill source hint line is shown.
- Focus on open is not the Attempt button.
- `closed` is emitted by Not yet and by Attempt.
- A meditation spot test (or extend the existing one): at QR 9th Layer bottleneck the attempt option's action is the new
  signal; a Mortal at the bottleneck still gets `GameState.attempt_breakthrough`.
Add the screen to the focus audit list if tests/unit/test_focus_audit.gd keeps one.
