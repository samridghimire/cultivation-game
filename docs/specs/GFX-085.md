# GFX-085: A festival square

Role: world-ui. Reference: Stardew Valley's festival layouts (the event happens on an open square, the crowd stands around
it). Mechanics only.

## Why (planner shot 2026-11-06 18:10, `screenshot_first_hour.gd -- --festival`, 1280x800)
On Dragon Boat day the contest stall is placed beside the first merchant spot (the Wandering Merchant in the middle of
Qingshi), so the stall, its caption "Dragon boat race today", the merchant's "!" badge, the Hunters' Bounty Board and a ring
of 4-6 villagers with "(Mortal)" / "(Qi Refining, 1st Layer)" labels all pile up in one 200 px square around the player's
start. The caption is cut to "Dragon" by Qin Yan's label (QA-198 noted the same).

## Changes
1. **Data:** regions.json regions may have an optional `festival_square: [x, y]` (document in `_doc`, validate in
   GameData: two numbers inside the region's bounds, not within 80 px of any place). Give it to every region that hosts a
   festival (Qingshi: an open patch of grass away from the board and stalls, e.g. east of the crossroads lower right; pick
   by looking at the region shot). Do not move any existing place.
2. **world.gd:** when a contest festival runs and the region has `festival_square`, the contest stall (the one whose
   caption is the crowd caption, today `_stalls[0]`) stands there instead of beside a merchant, and `_crowd_centre()`
   returns that square. Plain decorative stalls beside merchants stay. No `festival_square` = today's behaviour.
3. **Crowd:** `FestivalContest.crowd_positions`' avoid list also includes every place's position (not only the ones passed
   today) so no villager stands on a place or a stall (keep CROWD_CLEAR).
4. **Labels:** a *mortal* villager's near label no longer adds "(Mortal)" (src/world/interactables/npc.gd `label_text`:
   keep the realm suffix only for cultivators). The stall caption gets the same `spread_labels` pass as places and NPCs (or
   a z/priority above NPC labels) so no NPC label covers it.

## Tests (tests/unit)
- GameData rejects a festival_square outside the bounds or on top of a place.
- world integration (like the existing WU-457 crowd test): with a festival active in Qingshi the contest stall is at the
  square and every crowd position is >= CROWD_CLEAR from every place.
- npc label: a mortal near the player shows only the name; a Qi Refining villager shows the realm.
