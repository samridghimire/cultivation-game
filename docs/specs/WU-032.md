# WU-032: Confirm before costly or irreversible actions

Role: world-ui. Size: ~250 lines incl. tests.
Pattern to reuse: the beast Release button in src/ui/character_sheet.gd (`_release_pressed`, ~line 330): the first press
arms the button ("Confirm release?"), a second press does it, losing focus disarms it. For ChoiceMenu-based interactables
use the menu instead: the action opens a second ChoiceMenu (or the same one rebuilt) with "Yes, <do it>" / "No" and a
`description` saying what is lost. Gamepad focus must land on "No" first (safe default).

## Actions to guard
1. **Leave the sect** (src/world/interactables/sect_hall.gd ~19). Confirm text: "Leave <Sect>? You lose your rank (<rank>),
   <n> contribution and standing with the sect." (read rank/contribution from `GameState.player.sect`; check `Sects.leave`
   and `GameState.leave_sect` for what else is lost and mention reputation if it drops).
2. **Join a sect** (sect_hall.gd ~23). Confirm with a one-line summary: alignment range of the sect (righteous / neutral /
   demonic, from its min/max_alignment), monthly duty if the sect has one, and "You can leave later, at a cost."
3. **Feed all** and stone feeds on the artifact screen (src/ui/artifact_screen.gd ~133, ~164): arm-then-confirm button
   ("Confirm: feed N items?"). A single stone feed of under 100 stones needs no confirm.
4. **Using an item with `burn_lifespan`** or a negative `alignment` effect in the inventory (src/ui/inventory_screen.gd ~256):
   arm-then-confirm ("Burn N years? Press again").
5. **Equipping** an item that is blood-bound or drains lifespan (see game_state.gd ~465 and ~2020 for the flags it checks):
   arm-then-confirm.

## Tests
New tests/unit/test_confirm_actions.gd (look at test_character_sheet*.gd / test_focus_audit.gd for how screens are built in
tests): for each of 1-5, one press does not perform the action (state unchanged), the confirm performs it, and the cancel
path / focus loss leaves state unchanged. Sect hall: the first option after pressing Leave is focused and is "No".
