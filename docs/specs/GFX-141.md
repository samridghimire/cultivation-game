# GFX-141: Roads that are walked, not painted

Role: world-ui. Reference: Stardew Valley's and Wandering Sword's dirt roads (mechanics/look only, no art taken).

## Problem (planner shots 2026-11-14, 1280x720, `--season=Winter --region=qingshi_village,misty_forest,withered_bone_marsh`)
- In winter every dirt road shows a single row of dark dashes down its centre, like lane markings on a highway
  (Qingshi's crossroads, Misty Forest's trail). Cause: `Scenery.path_tiles` (src/core/systems/scenery.gd ~690-740) lays two
  rut rows at 30% / 62% of the width, then the GFX-124 winter rule drops every rut tile that is not within 20% of the centre,
  so only the evenly broken centre row survives.
- In all seasons the road's long edges are ruler straight (the path rect), so roads read as painted strips.
- The marsh's murky boardwalk is a solid full-width band with black bars at even spacing (the "missing" plank flag), so
  it reads as a railway track.

## Changes
1. **Ruts** (`Scenery.path_tiles`, dirt style): two wheel tracks that wander together (one shared seeded wobble per road,
   +-3 px, changing every 30-60 px), each track 2-3 px thick, broken by gaps of 6-20 px. Never a single centre row.
2. **Winter** (replace the GFX-124 drop rule): snow covers the road except the two wheel tracks, which stay as darker
   slush (base darkened ~0.15, not 0.22) and a scatter of footprints (`print` tiles) between them. Verges get a
   2-4 px bank of snow on both sides. No dashes.
3. **Ragged verges**: new `Scenery.road_verge(path_rect, seed) -> Array[Rect2]` returning small rects (2-6 px deep, 6-20 px
   long) that either bite into the road (grass colour) or spill out of it (road colour) along both long edges, every
   10-30 px. world.gd `_draw_road` draws them (snowy: snow colour on the grass side). Skip them where a place keepout,
   bridge or crossing road meets the edge (reuse the `crossings`/`bridges` lists).
4. **Marsh boardwalk** (plank tiles, `style == "bog"`): boards 2-4 px narrower than the path rect with the bog ground
   showing at both sides, each board's ends offset by 0-2 px (seeded), board gaps 1 px of dark water only; "missing"
   boards at most 1 in 14 and never two in a row; drop the even black bar look. Keep GFX-136's reed tufts and dark boards.
5. Keep paved (slab) streets as they are.

## Tests (tests/unit/test_road_look.gd)
- Winter dirt road in qingshi_village: every kept `rut`/slush tile lies off the centre line by >= 15% of the width, and
  there are tiles on both sides of the centre.
- No two consecutive rut tiles in one track have equal length and equal gap (not evenly dashed) across a 400 px road.
- `road_verge` returns rects on both long edges, none intersecting a place keepout, deterministic for a seed.
- Bog boardwalk: no two adjacent `missing` planks; missing share <= 1/12.
