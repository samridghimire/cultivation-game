# GFX-164: Myriad Peaks Ridge, ground and road (second pass)

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's mountain maps (look only: readable ground, a trail you would walk on).
Source: planner shots 2026-11-15 12:10, `--region=myriad_peaks_ridge` in Spring and Winter at 1280x720.

## What is wrong (after GFX-145/149/150/153/159)
1. **The ground reads as TV static.** The whole field is one dark slate with dense 1 px light speckle at an even density.
   GFX-159's slope bands are invisible at this zoom: the two tones are too close and the speckle drowns them.
2. **The main road is a flat pale band** (full map width at y ~360) with light rectangular blocks scattered on it. It has
   no edge, no verge and nothing that says "trail on a mountainside". Azure Peak's road has the same look.
3. **A soft grey translucent rectangle** sits in open ground at about (530, 460) in both seasons (a smudge patch, probably
   a `rust` ridge patch or a leftover placeholder rect). It reads as a rendering error.

## What to do
Files: `src/world/ground_tiles.gd` (`_scree_slopes` ~271, the speckle loop ~387), `src/world/world.gd` (road drawing for
the ridge's path kind, `_draw_ridge_patch` ~1901), `src/core/systems/scenery.gd` (pure helpers + tests).

1. **Ground.** Cut the light speckle to about a third of today's count on the ridge (map `scree_density` or a new
   per-map `speckle` factor read with a default, so other regions do not change), and draw it 1 px in a tone only a
   little lighter than the band under it (not near-white). Make the slope bands read: 3-4 broad seeded bands with a
   visible tone step (about 8-10% lightness between neighbours), with soft ragged band edges (no straight lines).
   In winter the bands are snow tones and the speckle is the dark rock showing through.
2. **Road.** Give the ridge (and Azure, if it shares the drawing) a trail: a slightly wobbling edge (reuse the
   GFX-156 marsh road edge helper if it is generic), a 3-5 px grit verge darker than the ground, worn stepping stones
   along the centre line in two sizes (lit top, dark foot), and no rectangular light blocks scattered across the band.
   Winter: a trodden darker line through snow with snow banked on both verges (like GFX-158's kerbs).
3. **The smudge.** Find what draws the grey rectangle at ~(530, 460) (render with `--region=myriad_peaks_ridge`, then
   comment out patch kinds one at a time). If it is a `rust` patch, draw rust as 3-6 small irregular reddish-brown
   spots like the lichen clumps, never a soft rect; if it is a place or placeholder, remove or replace it.

Put any new number rule in a pure static helper in Scenery with a unit test (e.g. band tone step, speckle count for a
density). Draw code stays seeded (no global rand).

## Acceptance
Render `--region=myriad_peaks_ridge` in Spring and Winter and `--region=azure_peak` in Spring at 1280x720, open all
three: the ground shows broad bands, not static; the road reads as a walked trail with verges; no grey rectangle.
Other regions' ground is unchanged (render Qingshi once to confirm). tools/test.sh.
