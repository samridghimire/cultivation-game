# C-392: A first fight in every starting region

Role: content (data only, plus tests). Reference: Wandering Sword's town odd jobs (mechanics only). Pattern: C-391 (b5756ff),
the Porters' Foreman's stray-dog chore in Fallen Star Market.

## Why
Planner shots 2026-11-18 18:10 (Autumn, a fresh mortal): Next steps in Azure Peak says "Easy fight: Wild Boar, Misty Forest
· 7d", in Withered Bone Marsh "Wild Boar, Misty Forest · 6d", and on Myriad Peaks Ridge there is no easy fight at all
(only an Iron-Back Boar (Deadly) on screen). A newcomer who walks there has nothing to fight for days.

## What to add
For each of `azure_peak`, `withered_bone_marsh`, `myriad_peaks_ridge`: one repeatable chore with an easy, non-lethal fight,
given by an NPC or place that already stands in that region (pick a fitting one; grep `deed_context` in data/npcs.json and
data/regions.json; add a `deed_context` to an existing named villager rather than inventing a new NPC if one fits):
- a mortal-tier foe in data/enemies.json modelled on `warehouse_stray_dog` (non-lethal, weak), themed to the region:
  e.g. a crag goat butting the pilgrims on Azure Peak's stair, a bog leech swarm on the marsh boardwalk, a rock-pecking
  ridge crow flock on the Ridge. Keep foe ids readable by GFX-199's beast keywords (goat/crow/leech may fall back to
  the generic body, which is fine; note it as a Follow-up for world-ui).
- a deed in data/deeds.json like `chore_clear_yard_dogs`: 1 day, `cooldown_days` 15-20, `enemy`, `witnessed`, small
  alignment (+2..+3), 6-10 spirit stones, a one-line `result` in the giver's voice.
## Balance targets
Rating "Easy" (>= 90 in 100) for a fresh mortal and for Qi Refining 1, asserted in tests/unit/test_first_hour.gd exactly as
C-391 did. The Next steps card in each region then shows an easy fight in that region with no code change (GUIDE-215/260).
Run `tools/balance.sh --check` (new foes and chores change measured numbers) and refresh the first-hour sections they explain.

## Acceptance
`screenshot_first_hour.gd -- --region=azure_peak,withered_bone_marsh,myriad_peaks_ridge` rendered and opened: each Next steps
card lists the new easy fight in its own region. tools/test.sh green.
