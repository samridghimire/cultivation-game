# GFX-127: A village people live in

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's lived-in villages and Stardew Valley's yards (mechanics and composition only, never
their art or layouts).

## Why (planner shots 2026-11-13 00:10, hud_arrival.png and region_qingshi_village.png at 1280x720)
Qingshi's four cottages (`map.landmarks` kind `house`), the temple, the workshop and the clinic stand alone on flat grass. No path
leads to any door, nothing sits beside them, and every cottage has the same blue roof. The village reads as buildings dropped on
a lawn, not a place people live.

## What to build
1. **Pure layout in core**: `Scenery.village_yards(region: Dictionary, size: Vector2) -> Dictionary` in
   `src/core/systems/scenery.gd`, built like GFX-126's `Scenery.market_props` (its own rng seeded from
   `hash("%s|yards" % region.id)`, no GameState, no global rng). Only for regions whose `map` has `"yards": true` (add it to
   qingshi_village in data/regions.json, document the field in the regions `_doc`, validate it is a bool in `Scenery.validate`).
   Returns:
   - `footpaths: Array[Dictionary]` `{rect: Rect2}`: for every `house` landmark and every place with a building look (the
     ones PixelPlace draws as a building: temple, workshop, clinic, sect hall, family home; read the look list in
     `src/world/pixel_place.gd`), one straight 10-12 px wide strip from the door (bottom centre of the building rect) to the
     nearest `map.paths` rect, first down then sideways (an L of at most two rects). Skip it when the door already touches a path
     or when the strip would be longer than 260 px.
   - `yards: Array[Dictionary]` `{rect: Rect2}`: a packed-earth patch 1.4x the house width x 24 px under each `house`.
   - `props: Array[Dictionary]` `{kind, rect}` beside each `house` (left or right side, seeded): 1-2 of `woodpile`, `water_jar`,
     `veg_row` (a 3-row vegetable bed 36x20), `washing_line` (two posts and a line 40 px wide), `bench`. No prop may overlap a
     path, place keepout (`Npcs.place_keepout`, grown by 10), any landmark rect, another prop, or the arrival ring; drop it if it
     does (use `Scenery.is_clear` and the keepouts `market_props` already builds).
   - `roof: Dictionary` landmark index -> Color: each `house` gets a seeded roof colour from a small palette
     (slate blue "4a6fa5", thatch "c9a35a", red tile "a8443a", grey tile "6b6f78"); the four Qingshi houses must not all share one.
2. **Draw it** in `src/world/world.gd`: cache the dict on region build (next to `_market_dress`), draw footpaths and yards in the
   ground pass right after roads (same walked-earth colours as GFX-124's dirt roads, slightly lighter edges), props after
   landmarks with the same drop shadow as `_draw_market_props`. Pass the roof colour to the `house` draw (pixel_landmark.gd)
   through an optional field; default stays the current colour so other regions do not change.
3. **Seasons**: under snow (`Scenery.is_snowy`) the yards and footpaths take the snowy road tint, veg rows show bare earth
   with a white cap, the washing line is empty in Winter. Autumn: veg rows orange/brown.

## Tests (tests/unit/test_village_yards.gd)
- Deterministic: two calls give the same dict.
- Every footpath rect touches a `map.paths` rect (grow by 1) and starts within 8 px of its building's bottom edge.
- No prop overlaps a path, a landmark, a place keepout or another prop.
- The four Qingshi house roof colours are not all equal; a region without `"yards"` returns empty arrays.
- `Scenery.validate` rejects `"yards": "yes"`.
