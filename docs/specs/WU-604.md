# WU-604: Welcomed at the hall (world-ui)

**After SECT-032** (needs `EventBus.sect_reported` and `Sects.needs_report`). If WU-603 (promotion mode of the same card) is on
main, build on its mode switch; if not, add the mode yourself and keep it easy for WU-603 to slot in.
**Reference:** Tale of Immortal's sect entry (receiving your first supplies at the sect), mechanics only, never its layout.

## 1. Sect cards wait their turn (WU-602 Follow-up)
`hud.gd ~2142` opens `_sect_card` at once on `sect_joined`, i.e. over the still-open hall choice menu. Route sect cards through
WU-601's FIFO: append `{"kind": "sect_join", "sect_id": id}` (and below `"sect_report"`) to `_pending_moments` instead of opening
directly, and play them from the same place the other moments play (`_play_next_moment` ~1919 / `_update_modal` ~2561), so the
card appears right after the choice menu closes. Joining happens with `keep_open: true` in sect_hall.gd: make sure the hall menu
closes (or the card plays when it closes) rather than the card waiting forever; the simplest fix is to drop `keep_open` from
the "Yes, join" and "Report" actions. Test in tests/unit/test_hud_moments.gd: a sect_join emitted while the choice menu is
visible does not show the card until the menu closes.

## 2. The welcome card
On `EventBus.sect_reported(sect_id, items)` queue `{"kind": "sect_report", ...}`; when played open SectCard in a welcome mode:
- Title: sect name with the code-drawn banner (as WU-602), subtitle "Welcome to the hall, <rank name>".
- The steward's line in quotes (the line SECT-032 posts: capture it in the moment dict, like WU-601 captures the tend line;
  add `Sects.welcome_line(c, data)` in core if you need it twice).
- "Received:" then one row per item with its `ItemIcon` and "2 x Qi Gathering Pill".
- "This hall offers:" up to 4 short rows built in a new pure helper `Guidance.hall_services(c, data, region_id) -> PackedStringArray`
  in guidance.gd (unit-tested): "Mission board: N open now", "Lecture: <name>, <when>" (Sects.check_lecture reason or "today"),
  "Treasury: <cheapest claimable item> for N contribution", "Next rank: <rank_needs joined>" (omit lines that do not apply).
- Continue button with focus; confirm/cancel close; blocks the HUD like the join card.

## 3. Polish on the join card
Perk lines read as raw keys ("gather qi x1.5"): capitalise the first letter of each perk line and write the qi perk as
"Gather qi 1.5x faster" (change the text where `Sects.membership_perks` builds it only if no test or other UI depends on the old
wording; otherwise transform in the card).
