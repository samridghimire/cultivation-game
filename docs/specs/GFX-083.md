# GFX-083: Festival stalls that look like a festival

Role: world-ui. After FEST-012 (on main). Reference: Stardew Valley's festival stalls and decorations, mechanics only.

## Why
FEST-012 added four `festival_only` merchants (data/regions.json: Dragon Boat Dumpling Stall and Lantern Sweets Stall in
qingshi_village, Mooncake Stall at the market, Qingming Offering Stall at azure_peak). They draw as the ordinary merchant
stall. WU-097's FestivalStall decoration (src/world/festival_stall.gd, `decor_for`) only knows "willow" (Qingming) and
"lanterns" (everything else). FEST-012 Follow-up: "WU art for the stalls".

## Spec
1. `FestivalStall.decor_for(festival_id)` gains one look per festival: `dragon_boat` (a short boat prow with a dragon head
   and a drum beside the stall, green/red), `lanterns` (Lantern Festival: two strings of red lanterns over the awning, soft
   glow), `moon` (Mid-Autumn: a pale moon disc on a pole and osmanthus sprigs, gold/cream), `willow` (Qingming: willow
   branches and a kite on a string, green). Keep the existing ones' behaviour for other callers. Draw with `_draw()`
   primitives at the existing pixel scale (Sprites key `festival_stall_<look>` first if data/sprites.json maps one, else the
   placeholder), static under reduce_motion (`MotionFx.reduced()`), gentle sway/glow otherwise.
2. A `festival_only` merchant (src/world/interactables/merchant.gd has `festival_only`) draws its awning in its place
   `color` with a scalloped festival trim and gets the matching decoration beside it (reuse FestivalStall; place it so it
   does not cover the label or the [E] prompt, LabelLayout already avoids labels).
3. Screenshot script: `--festival-stall=<event id>` in tests/sim/screenshot_first_hour.gd jumps to that festival's first
   day in its region, stands the player 120 px from the stall and saves `festival_stall_<event id>.png` (if QA-198's
   `--festival` landed, reuse its jump helper).
4. Unit test: `decor_for` returns a distinct look for each of the four festival ids in data/world_events.json with
   `festival: true`, and every festival_only place's festival has a look other than the default.

## View (required)
`xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1280x800x24" tools/godot.sh --path . --rendering-driver opengl3 --resolution 1280x800 -s
res://tests/sim/screenshot_first_hour.gd -- --festival-stall=dragon_boat_festival` and the same for
`mid_autumn_festival` (~3 min each). Describe both in the commit: the stall reads as festive, label and prompt clear.
