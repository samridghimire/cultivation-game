# GFX-133: Ground without boxes (world-ui)

Reference: Wandering Sword's village squares and Stardew Valley's worn paths: packed earth fades into grass with a ragged
edge, never a straight one (mechanics only, no art copied).

## What is wrong (planner shots 2026-11-13 12:10, 1280x720)
- `first_hour/hud_arrival.png` (spring): Qingshi's new village square (GFX-131, `map.square` + `Scenery.village_square`)
  is a flat brown **rectangle** with straight edges and square corners, cut into four by the roads. The cottage yards
  (GFX-127) are pale straight-edged rectangles too. Up close it looks like a board game, not a village.
- With `-- --season=Winter`: the square, the worn ground around the bounty board/herb seller and every cottage yard are
  hard-edged pale blue-white rectangles on the snow, the most visible shapes on screen.
- GFX-130 Follow-up: the per-pixel ground build time was never measured.

## Changes (src/core/systems/scenery.gd, src/world/ground_tiles.gd or wherever GFX-130/131 draw; grep `village_square`, `patch_shade`, `yard`)
1. **Ragged edges.** Square, worn-ground and yard fills get an irregular outline: per edge, a seeded noise offset of
   4-14 px in and out (sample every 8 px, smooth), and rounded corners (radius 12-24 px). Use the region seed so the
   outline is stable across reloads. Grass tufts (2-4 per 64 px of edge) sit along the edge, half over it.
2. **A centre that reads as a square.** Inside Qingshi's square, a loose ring of 10-16 flagstones round the big tree's
   stone ring (irregular sizes 10-18 px, 2-3 grey tones, gaps of grass/earth) and 2-3 darker worn tracks where the roads
   enter. The rest stays packed earth with GFX-130's patch shading so it isn't one flat brown.
3. **Winter.** Square, worn ground and yards under snow use the same ragged outline; the snow over them is only slightly
   greyer/bluer than the snow round them (contrast <= 12% in lightness), with a few dark earth specks where feet wore it
   through. No pale rectangles.
4. **Measure.** Time the first build of Qingshi's ground (spring and winter) with `Time.get_ticks_usec()` in a test or the
   shot script and put the numbers in the commit. If either is over 250 ms on the cloud container, cache the built image
   per (region, season) the way GFX-130 already does and say so.
