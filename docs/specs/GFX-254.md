# GFX-254: The contest, staged

Reference: Tale of Immortal's sect competitions (mechanics: a ladder of bouts, a crowd) and Wandering Sword's readable martial
presentation (look only). Role: world-ui. Size: ~300 lines. Replaces WU-620 (the rival staged is part 4 here).

## Why
Planner shot 2026-11-24 12:10 (`--sect-contest=azure_cloud_sect`): `SectContestCard` is a banner plus four wrapped text lines.
The trial card (GFX-250 `TrialCard.TrialArena`, WU-616 lost pose) already has a 480x128 PixelFigure arena; the contest, the
sect's biggest yearly moment, deserves the same: the platform, banners, the player facing the round's foe.

## Build
1. `src/ui/sect_contest_card.gd`: add an inner class `ContestArena extends SectCard.SectVignette` (same W/H/GROUND as
   TrialArena, read `src/ui/trial_card.gd` ~220-250 and `SectCard.SectVignette` in `src/ui/sect_card.gd` for `draw_figure`,
   `_player()`, `_disciple()`, `_props()`, `color`).
   `_draw()`: dusk sky; a raised wooden platform (a lighter plank rect 12 px tall across the middle 300 px, posts under it);
   two banner poles at the platform corners in the sect colour (the same robe colour `ContestStage` uses for its banners,
   see `src/world/contest_stage.gd`); 3-4 small spectator figures (scale 2.0) below the platform edge on each side; the player
   (scale 4, facing right) and the foe (scale 4, facing left) on the platform.
   Fields: `foe_look: Dictionary` (a PixelFigure look; default `_disciple({"robe": <darker sect colour>})`), `mode: String`
   ("ready" | "won" | "lost").
   - "won": the foe kneels back a step with the sword on the ground (mirror WU-616's player pose), the player stands with the
     sword raised (a vertical 4x22 rect above the hand).
   - "lost": exactly the TrialArena result pose (player kneels, sword on the ground), foe sword pointing down.
2. Put the arena at the top of the card in `_init()` (as TrialCard does: `custom_minimum_size = Vector2(W, H)`), above the ladder
   lines. `open()` sets mode "ready" and the first foe; `open_result(sect_name, info, color)` sets mode from `info["won"]` and the
   foe of that bout. Card width may grow to fit the 480 px arena plus margins; check it still fits 1280x720 (QA-247 renders it).
3. Make the ladder lines read as a ladder: one row per round, the current round highlighted (UIStyle accent), won rounds with a
   check glyph and dimmed, the prize in a smaller muted font on the same row. Keep `all_text()` and `ladder_lines()` working:
   existing tests in `tests/unit/test_sect_contest_card.gd` (or wherever SectContestCard is tested; grep) must stay green, update
   expected strings only where the format change requires it.
4. **The rival** (was WU-620; SECT-044 is on main): when the bout's foe carries `"person"` (see `GameState._contest_foe` and
   `_note_contest_rival`), the arena's `foe_look` is the rival's look (the same helper the family/people screens use for a
   CharacterData; grep `look_of` / `PlayerLook.of`) and the ladder row reads `"Round 3: your rival <name>"`; the result text of a
   bout against the rival names them ("You defeat your rival <name>." / "<name> bests you."). The info dict emitted by
   `sect_contest_bout` may need `"rival": true` and the look: add it in GameState (UI-only data, no rule change).

## Tests
