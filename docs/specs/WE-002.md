# WE-002: Festivals (world events in fixed months, favor bonus)

**Why.** Seasons now change herbs and the HUD; give each year a few dates worth planning around, and give the social
side (chat, gifts, courtship) a reason to happen at a time and place.

## Rules (src/core/systems/world_events.gd, data/world_events.json `_doc`)
- Optional `"months": [int, ...]` (1-12, as `Calendar.month_of` numbers them; check whether it is 0- or 1-based and use the
  same) on a world event: `WorldEvents.roll` only considers the event when `Calendar.month_of(total_days)` is listed.
  With `monthly_chance: 1.0` and one month this makes a yearly fixed-date event.
- Optional `"festival": true`: purely a marker for UI/news (WU-072); `WorldEvents.is_festival(data, event_id) -> bool`.
- New modifier `modifiers.favor_mult` (float >= 1.0): `WorldEvents.favor_multiplier(data, active, region_id) -> float`
  via `_product`. In `GameState.chat` (~1035) and `GameState.give_gift` (~1111) multiply `int(result["favor"])` by the
  multiplier for `current_region` (roundi) **before** `NpcClans.scaled_favor` so the existing caps still clamp it; when
  the multiplier is > 1 add " The festival warms the mood." to the posted line.
- Validation (`WorldEvents.validate`): months are ints in range and non-empty when present; favor_mult >= 1.0.
- `Guidance.journal` Opportunities: an active festival in the current region or a nearby one (`Exploration.is_nearby`)
  gets a line "<Event name> in <Region>: <N> days left" (reuse the existing joinable-event line style if one exists).
- Add one festival to data/world_events.json so the system is exercised: `lantern_festival`, regions ["qingshi_village"],
  months [10] (the first month of winter; `Calendar.month_of` is 1-based and months 10-12 are Winter), monthly_chance 1.0, min_days 7, max_days 10,
  festival true, modifiers {"favor_mult": 2.0, "price_mult": 0.9}, start/end text in genre voice (paper lanterns on the
  river, villagers burning incense for the ancestors). C-047 adds more and festival encounters.

## Tests (tests/unit/test_world_events.gd)
- An event with `months` never starts in other months over 24 months of rolls (seeded rng) and always starts in its month
  with chance 1.0.
- favor_multiplier is 2.0 in qingshi_village while the lantern festival is active, 1.0 elsewhere and after it ends.
- GameState: chatting with a Qingshi NPC during the festival gains (about) twice the favor of a normal chat, never above
  `chat_max_favor`.
- Validator rejects month 13 and favor_mult 0.5.
- Active festival instances survive save/load (world_events already saved; assert it).
