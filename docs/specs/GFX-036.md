# GFX-036: The fallen beast lies down, the backdrop reads as distance, storms show in shots

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's duel framing (mechanics of presentation only: a far layer, a near layer,
fighters on the ground line). Planner shots 2026-10-31 12:15 (`screenshot_first_hour.gd -- --storm`, 1280x800).

## What the shots show
1. `combat_report.png`: the defeated Wild Boar is drawn **standing on its tail in mid-air** (a tall dark shape at
   ~x 770, y 185-255, its feet ~40 px above the ground band), with "Defeated" under the grass. GFX-033's "fallen fighters
   lie on their side" rotates the foe by `_tilt[1]` about its *feet*, so a quadruped (wider than tall) swings up into the air.
2. The Qingshi "roofs" silhouette is a row of identical evenly spaced green blocks that read as hedges, not roofs.
   The sky above is a flat empty gradient; the fighters use only the bottom ~third of a 180 px stage.
3. `--storm` shots show an ordinary sunny day: the flag sets `world._weather_kind = "storm"` once, but the first action
   that advances the clock calls `_on_days_advanced`, which recomputes the weather from `Weather.of` and drops the storm.

## Changes
`src/ui/duel_stage.gd`
- A fallen **beast** (`_foe_beast` and who == 1) lies on its side on the ground line: draw it with the texture rect
  rotated about the body's centre and then moved down so its lowest edge sits on `base_y` (equivalently: use the existing
  `on_back` flip path for beasts, which keeps the feet on the ground). A fallen **person** keeps today's tilt but must also
  end with its lowest point within 2 px of `base_y`. Put the geometry in a pure static helper
  `fallen_rect(tex_size: Vector2, feet: Vector2, beast: bool) -> Rect2` (the axis-aligned bounds after the fall) so it is
  testable without drawing.
- "Defeated" is drawn above the fallen body (just over its top edge), not under the grass.
- Backdrop: the silhouette layer gets variation from a seeded hash of the region id (no rng): widths 0.7-1.3x, heights
  0.6-1.2x, irregular gaps, and for `roofs` an actual roof shape (a trapezoid with upturned eave tips, a dark wall below).
  Colour: lerp the silhouette towards the sky's horizon colour by ~45% so it reads as distance (haze), never pure green.
  Add one far layer behind it (low hills or a mountain line, 70% haze) in every region kind. Optionally 2-3 soft clouds
  (ellipses, 15% alpha white) in clear-sky seasons; none on rain/storm days (darken the sky by the weather tint instead).
- Raise the ground line or scale fighters so they occupy the middle of the stage: a standing fighter's head is at ~40-50%
  of the stage height (keep `Sprites.scale()` integer zoom; move `base_y` up rather than stretching).

`src/world/world.gd`
- Add `force_weather(kind: String) -> void` (doc comment: screenshots/tests only): stores `_forced_weather`; when it is not
  empty, `_build_season_tint`/`_on_days_advanced` use it instead of `Weather.of`. `tests/sim/screenshot_first_hour.gd`
  `--storm` calls it instead of setting `_weather_kind` directly. Do **not** change `Weather.of` or any rule.

## Tests (tests/unit/test_duel_stage*.gd or a new test_gfx_036.gd)
- `fallen_rect` for a beast texture 32x20 at feet (100, 150): bottom == 150 (+-2), height <= 20*z + 2 (it lies flat, not taller).
- Same for a person: bottom within 2 px of feet.y.
- Silhouette shapes for two different region ids differ; the same id gives the same shapes twice.
- `force_weather("storm")` survives `GameClock.advance(1)` (the world's `_weather_kind` stays "storm").
