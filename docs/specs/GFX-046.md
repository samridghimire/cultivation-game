# GFX-046: Jetties on the water, a marsh with dead trees, a quieter bog

Role: world-ui. Follow-ups of GFX-044 and GFX-045, from the planner's 1280x800 arrival shots of 2026-11-01 12:10
(`screenshot_first_hour.gd -- --region=<id>`).
Reference: **Wandering Sword** (docks that reach into the water, areas whose mood is told by their trees). Mechanics and
readability only; no art taken.

## What the shots show
1. **Jetties float on grass.** The `jetty` landmark copies its fishing place's `pos`, so it sits 40-60 px below its pond:
   Qingshi pond at [330, 230], Village Pond Jetty at [330, 310]; Misty pond [860, 380], Misty Pond Jetty [860, 450];
   Bog Pond Shore [430, 308] against the pond at [400, 180]. A dock that does not touch water reads as a bench.
2. **The marsh shows no dead trees.** `withered_bone_marsh` has `decor.tree_kinds {dead 5, willow 1}` but only 18 decor trees
   over a large map, and **6 hand-placed `pine` landmarks**, which are what you see: blue-green pines in a dead marsh.
3. **The bog ground is noisy.** Dark dashes everywhere read as dirt specks on a grid, and two roamers (an ape and a boar)
   stack on one spot at the bottom of the shot (that part is WU-426).

## Build
1. **Jetty meets the water** (world.gd where the jetty landmark is added, ~line 885, and/or `PixelLandmark._jetty`):
   when a fishing place has a `pond` landmark whose rect, grown by 160 px, contains the place's pos, place the jetty so it
   runs from the pond's nearest rim toward the place: long side perpendicular to the shore, planks overlapping the water
   by 6-8 px, posts in the water drawn darker. Otherwise keep today's placement. Keep the fishing place's interact area
   where it is (the player walks onto the jetty end). Streams (Cloud-Stream Ford) keep today's placement.
2. **Landmark trees follow the region:** `map.landmarks` `pine` entries accept an optional `tree_kind`
   (`round|pine|willow|dead|bamboo`, default `pine`; document in regions.json `_doc`, validate with `Scenery.TREE_KINDS`)
   and draw that kind with the existing decor tree painters (keep them solid). Set the marsh's 6 to `dead` (or 4 dead,
   2 willow) and raise its `decor.trees` 18 -> 30. Myriad Peaks Ridge keeps pines.
3. **Calmer bog:** in `GroundTiles` bog style, halve the dark dash density again, vary dash length (2-5 px) and alpha
   (0.25-0.5) per tile variant, and add 2-3 small puddle patches per 4 tiles in a slightly lighter, bluish tone, so the
   ground reads as wet, not dirty. The other ground styles must not change (compare a Qingshi shot before/after).

## Tests
- A jetty for a place within 160 px of a pond overlaps the pond rect (by 4+ px) and does not overlap the place's sign/label.
- `tree_kind` on a landmark outside `Scenery.TREE_KINDS` fails validation; a `dead` landmark draws (non-empty texture).
- Marsh fixed-seed: the arrival view (spawn +- 640x400) contains at least 3 dead trees (decor or landmark).
