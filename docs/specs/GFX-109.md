# GFX-109: Peaks that look like peaks

Role: world-ui. Reference: Wandering Sword's mountain maps (mechanics only: a mountain area reads as a mountain because
the ground has height, shelves of rock with a lit top and a shaded face, snow lying in the lee of rocks, and paths that
wind between them). Take nothing else: no names, layouts or art.

## Why (planner shot 2026-11-09 18:10, `screenshot_first_hour.gd -- --region=myriad_peaks_ridge`)
Myriad Peaks Ridge is the late-game mountain region, but at 1280x800 it is a flat blue-grey plain with grey speckle,
a handful of small rocks and three pines. Nothing says "ridge" or "high and cold". Azure Peak (also `ground_style:
scree`) has the same problem in a milder form. The village, forest and market already look like what they are.

## What to build
1. **Data.** New optional regions.json `map.relief`:
   `{"ledges": int 0..12, "snow": float 0..1, "boulders": int 0..12}`. Document it in regions.json `_doc` and validate it
   in `GameData._validate()` (ints in range, snow in 0..1, unknown keys rejected). Set it on
   `myriad_peaks_ridge` (ledges 8, snow 0.6, boulders 8) and `azure_peak` (ledges 5, snow 0.15, boulders 6).
   Regions without `relief` look exactly as today.
2. **Pure layout** in `src/core/systems/scenery.gd` (no Nodes):
   `static func relief_layout(region: Dictionary) -> Array[Dictionary]`, seeded only from the region id
   (`hash(region_id + "|relief")` into a local RandomNumberGenerator, never global randomness). Each entry is
   `{kind: "ledge"|"boulders"|"drift", rect: Rect2, seed: int}`.
   - Ledges: 60-180 px wide, 18-36 px tall rock shelves.
   - Boulder clusters: 3-5 rocks of mixed size in a 40-70 px area.
   - Drifts: soft white patches 30-90 px wide on the lee (south) side of ledges and boulders, about `snow` of them get one.
   - Keep everything off: paths (`map.paths`, plus 12 px), place rects (pos/size, plus 16 px), landmarks
     (`landmark_rects`), the spawn point and travel points (radius 60), NPC spots (radius 24), and the 24 px map border.
     Try up to 30 positions per item, drop it if none fits (never overlap).
3. **Drawing** in `src/world/world.gd` `_draw()`, after the paths and before the landmarks (so places and props sit on
   top). Code-drawn pixel art at `Sprites.scale()` like PixelPlace templates, crisp and integer-scaled:
   - Ledge: a lit top band (ground colour lightened 0.25, 3-4 px), a darker vertical face (darkened 0.35) with 2-3
     crack lines, a 1 px outline, and an uneven top edge (step the height every 6-10 px from `seed`).
   - Boulders: rounded rocks using the region's `rock_color` with a light top-left and a dark bottom-right pixel run.
   - Drift: off-white (`e8eef4`) blobs with a 1 px blue-grey shade line on their lower edge; in Winter every ledge gets
     one, in Summer drifts shrink to half (use `GroundTiles.ground_season`).
   Decor already placed by `Scenery.place` must not sit on a ledge: filter `_decor` against the relief rects in
   `_build_decor` (like `SectCourt.filter_decor`).
4. **Collision:** none. Relief is visual only; the player walks over it (a later task may block ledges).

## Tests (`tests/unit/test_relief.gd`, new)
- `relief_layout` is deterministic (two calls equal) and empty for a region without `map.relief`.
- For both regions: no relief rect intersects a path (+12), a place rect (+16), a landmark rect, the spawn/travel
  points (radius 60) or the border; counts never exceed the data values.
- Validation rejects `ledges: 13`, `snow: 1.5` and an unknown key.
