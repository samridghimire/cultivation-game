# WU-573: A world you can hear

Role: world-ui. Planner 2026-11-19 18:10. Reference: Stardew Valley's and Wandering Sword's region ambience (birdsong, water,
wind, a market murmur under the music), mechanics only. No binary assets: everything is synthesized in code like REL-004.

## Why
Today the world is silent apart from UI clicks, chimes, fight hits and the guqin music loop (src/autoload/audio.gd). Walking
through Qingshi, standing by a stream in the rain or meditating at Azure Peak sounds the same. A soft ambience bed per region
and weather, plus a handful of action sounds, is the cheapest big win in game feel left.

## What to build (keep it under ~400 changed lines)
1. **Data** (Rule 2): optional `map.ambience` per region in data/regions.json:
   `{bed: village|forest|marsh|mountain|city, water (optional bool, default false: a stream/pond murmur layer)}`.
   Set it for the six regions: qingshi_village village + water, misty_forest forest, fallen_star_market city,
   azure_peak mountain + water, withered_bone_marsh marsh + water, myriad_peaks_ridge mountain.
   Document the field in the regions `_doc` (one sentence) and validate it in `GameData._validate()` (unknown bed or key
   rejected). A region without it gets `village` (safe default).
2. **Pure selection**, testable without audio: `static func ambience_for(region: Dictionary, weather: String, season: String) -> Dictionary`
   in a new `src/core/systems/soundscape.gd` (`class_name Soundscape`, RefCounted, no Nodes). Returns
   `{layers: Array[String], accents: Array[String], accent_every: Vector2}`:
   - layers: the bed's loop (`amb_wind_soft` for mountain/forest, `amb_village` a very low murmur for village/city with
     `amb_crowd` added for city, `amb_marsh` for marsh) plus `amb_water` when water, plus `amb_rain` on rain, `amb_rain` +
     `amb_wind_hard` on storm, `amb_wind_hard` instead of the soft wind on snow in mountain regions.
   - accents (one-shots fired at a random gap in `accent_every` seconds, e.g. 5-14 s): `bird` in spring/summer on clear or
     cloudy days (village, forest, mountain), `crow` in autumn/winter, `frog` in marsh spring/summer, `insect` in summer
     forest/marsh, `bell` (a distant temple bell, every 30-60 s) in mountain regions; none on storm days.
3. **Synthesis** in src/autoload/audio.gd: build the loops lazily (first use), mono, MIX_RATE 11025, 3-4 s each,
   `loop_mode = AudioStreamWAV.LOOP_FORWARD`, with the start and end crossfaded (or the noise made periodic) so there is no
   click at the seam. Brown/pink noise with a slow amplitude wobble makes water and wind; filtered noise with a 4-8 Hz
   wobble makes the crowd; very soft band-limited noise the village. Accents are short RECIPES-style one-shots (a two-note
   rising chirp `bird`, a rasping 2-pulse `crow`, a low 2-pulse `frog`, a 6 kHz trill `insect`, a 330 Hz decaying sine with
   a soft overtone `bell`). Use the passed-in or Audio's own `RandomNumberGenerator` (seeded), never global `randf()`.
4. **Playback**: a new bus `Ambience` (add it to default_bus_layout.tres next to SFX/Music, or create it at runtime in
   Audio._ready if missing) with up to 4 looping AudioStreamPlayers; on `EventBus.region_changed` and on a day change
   (GameClock.days_advanced, for weather) recompute `Soundscape.ambience_for(...)` and crossfade (1.5 s) layers that change;
   accents play through a Timer at random gaps. Paused when the window loses focus (like the music). Silent on the title
   screen and in the main menu.
5. **Action sounds** (new RECIPES, gain <= 0.3, all routed through the SFX bus):
   - `step_snow` / `step_dirt`: a very soft tick every second footprint the Footprints node (src/world/footprints.gd)
     lays (snow crunch on snowy ground, a dull pat on roads); nothing on grass. Never more than 4 per second.
   - `meditate_hum`: a slow swelling low hum when a cultivate/seclusion TimeSkipOverlay opens (GFX-213's hook), `bell` when it
     finishes.
   - `coin`: merchant buy/sell (replace the two `chime_progress` calls in src/world/interactables/merchant.gd ~182/189).
   - `rustle`: a gather/forage pick (ForagePickup and gather places); `splash`: a fishing trip starts.
6. **Settings**: an "Ambience" volume slider in src/ui/settings_screen.gd beside Music and SFX; `ambience_volume` in
   src/autoload/settings.gd (`VOLUME_BUSES`, default 0.6), so old settings files load with the default. Keyboard and gamepad
   focus works like the other sliders.
7. **For the owner to listen**: `tests/sim/dump_audio.gd` (headless) writes every RECIPE and every ambience loop as
   `user://audio/<name>.wav` (AudioStreamWAV.save_to_wav). Mention the folder in the commit message.

## Tests (tests/unit/test_soundscape.gd)
- Every region in regions.json yields at least one layer; qingshi clear spring -> village + water layers and a `bird` accent;
  withered_bone_marsh rain -> marsh + water + rain, no bird; a storm day has no accents; fallen_star_market includes `amb_crowd`.
- GameData rejects `map.ambience.bed = "ocean"` and an unknown key.
- Every new RECIPE and every loop name Soundscape can return builds a non-empty AudioStreamWAV in Audio; loops have
  `loop_mode` set and loop_end == sample count.
- Settings: a settings dict without `ambience_volume` loads with 0.6.
