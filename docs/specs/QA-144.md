# QA-144: What do spare stones buy? Qi pills in the first year

## Why
Planner trace 2026-10-23 06:10 (seed 59, `-- 1 12 --curious --trace --seed=59`): the curious player never leaves Qingshi
and ends month 12 at Qi Refining 6th layer holding **349 spirit stones** (10-seed median 230). The only hint about
spending is the armor hint (a 10-day trip, GEAR-001). Yet Qingshi's Wandering Merchant (no `stock_tags`, so it sells every
untagged item) sells the **Qi Gathering Pill for 15 stones (150 qi, the hint calls it ~11 days of meditation)**, the
Superior Qi Gathering Pill (45, 400 qi) and the Spirit Gathering Pill (90, 900 qi). 349 stones of Qi Gathering Pills is
~23 pills, ~3,450 qi: roughly a full layer at QR 6. No hint says so, and the sim never buys one.

Before any hint points at this (GUIDE-126), measure what it does to the first year, because pacing is the owner's open
question (DESIGN.md F-005d) and the Foundation pill (300 stones) competes for the same stones.
